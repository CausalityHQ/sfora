# Verdict: **DEAD**

Reached independently of the frozen author's NONE. I agree the method does not survive, but **on different and stronger grounds**: the one component the frozen text defends as "genuinely good" — the rank-exclusion theorem — is **false as stated**, and its failure mode is exactly the degeneracy it was built to exclude. That is a substantive defect requiring repair, not a forecasting shortfall.

---

## 1. What actually checks out

Stating this first because the kills depend on the rest being right.

- **Bounds.** $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}=\frac{\kappa}{d}\sum_i(\hat\lambda_i+\kappa)^{-1}$ with $\hat\lambda_i\ge0,\ \sum\hat\lambda_i=d$. Convexity of $x\mapsto(x+\kappa)^{-1}$ ⟹ minimum $\kappa/(1+\kappa)=1/3$ at $\hat\lambda\equiv1$. Supremum at rank-1: $\frac{d-1}{d}+\frac{\kappa}{d(d+\kappa)}\approx0.998<1$. Stated interval is correct (sup not attained).
- **Gradient.** With $s=\operatorname{tr}\tilde\Sigma$, $d\hat\Sigma=\frac{d}{s}[d\tilde\Sigma-\frac{\hat\Sigma}{d}\operatorname{tr}(d\tilde\Sigma)]$, giving $\partial\mathcal L/\partial\tilde\Sigma=\frac{d}{s}[G-\frac1d\operatorname{tr}(G\hat\Sigma)I]$, $G=-\frac{\kappa}{d}(\hat\Sigma+\kappa I)^{-2}$. **Algebraically correct.** Sign analysis also correct: $g_i+c$ is negative on small $\hat\lambda_i$, positive on large — descent transfers within-class variance from occupied to starved directions, trace-preserving.
- **Scale invariance** is real: $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}(c\tilde\Sigma)=\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}(\tilde\Sigma)$.
- **Rank arithmetic.** rank $\le k$ ⟹ $\mathcal L\ge(d-k)/d$; $413/512=0.8066$ ✓, $415/512=0.8105$ for Cars ✓. $k=C{-}1$ for a posterior ✓. Vacuity on SOP ($k=11{,}317$) and In-Shop ($k=3{,}996$) ✓.
- **$\kappa=0.5$ is a well-chosen threshold**, not a fudge: at trace-normalized mean eigenvalue 1, a direction only registers as alive at $\hat\lambda\gtrsim0.5$. This defeats the obvious attack that small parameter drift across the EMA window fakes rank — drift adds $O(\epsilon^2)$ eigenvalues, which still read as dead. Credit where due.
- **EMA as a population estimator is defensible.** Over $1/m=20$ steps the estimator sees ~2,560 images spanning most CUB train classes; $n_{\text{eff}}/d\approx0.27$, moderate Marchenko–Pastur distortion. Not rank-starved.
- **Second view is legal.** "One view" is an inference-time constraint; a train-time augmented view with zero deployment cost does not violate it.

## 2. The theorem is false, and false in the load-bearing direction

**Claim:** $f=g\circ s$ for *any* $k$-dimensional statistic $s$ ⟹ $\operatorname{rank}\Sigma_w\le k$.

**This holds only if $g$ is affine.** The image of a nonlinear $g$ is a $k$-dimensional *manifold* in $\mathbb R^d$, not a $k$-dimensional linear subspace, and the covariance of a distribution supported on a $k$-manifold has rank up to $d$.

Counterexample with $k=1$: let $s(x)=t\in[0,1]$ be any scalar that varies within class, and $g(t)=(a_1P_1(t),\dots,a_{512}P_{512}(t))$ with $P_i$ Legendre. $\operatorname{Cov}(g(t))$ is diagonal, full rank 512, and the $a_i$ can be tuned to make it isotropic — driving $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}$ to its **global minimum $1/3$** for an encoder that is a function of a single scalar. $\ell_2$ normalization does not rescue this (it preserves rank generically; it only preserves the bound in the affine case, where $\operatorname{span}\{z\}\subseteq\operatorname{col}(A)$).

The instantiation that matters: let $s$ be the $(C{-}1)$-dim train-class posterior and $g$ any nonlinear expansion — a random-weight two-layer ReLU map, polynomial features, or simply the fact that $\phi_\theta$ is nonlinear downstream of any bottleneck. Then $z=h(s(x))$, so by data processing $I(z;x)\le I(s(x);x)$: **zero information beyond the train-class posterior**, while $\Sigma_w$ is generically full rank and the barrier is minimizable to $1/3$.

Consequences:

1. The exclusion theorem excludes only **linear replication**. The frozen text's own example list ("a scalar shortcut replicated into 512 coordinates") is the one case it covers; "the train-class posterior," "the logits," and "any simplex-ETF code" read out through a nonlinear head are **not** covered.
2. This is strictly stronger than the frozen author's primary kill. They conceded exclusion and asked whether the *filler* is useful. There need be no filler: the barrier is satisfiable by nonlinear re-embedding of the same sufficient statistic, at zero informational cost, and $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}$ **actively rewards** doing so.
3. The frozen text's "generalizable residue" is therefore also wrong. Its Replication Lemma claims dimension counting is the one exception to the "functionals of the law of $(z,y)$ cannot force retention" argument. **Rank is not an exception** — rank is not invariant under the nonlinear reparametrizations the network is free to apply, so it is not a lower bound on retained information about $x$.

## 3. Further formal degeneracies

**(a) The finite-batch floor sits above the exclusion threshold.** $\operatorname{rank}\Sigma_w^{(t)}\le B-C_b=96$ ⟹ every per-batch measurement satisfies $\mathcal L\ge(512-96)/512=0.8125$, which **exceeds** the CUB shortcut threshold 0.8066 and the Cars threshold 0.8105. The certificate is therefore inert on any instantaneous measurement and depends wholly on the EMA — i.e. on a time-average over $\{\theta_{t'}\}$, not on the deployed encoder. Nothing in the method certifies the shipped model.

**(b) Scale invariance severs the method from its own motivating mechanism.** Set within-class residuals $r_i=\epsilon n_i$ with $n_i$ isotropic. Then $\hat\Sigma\to I$ and $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}\to1/3$ — the **global minimum — under total within-class variability collapse** $\epsilon\to0$. The neural-collapse-transfer literature the method invokes measures NC1 as a *ratio* of within- to between-class scatter, a magnitude quantity; $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}$ regularizes only spectral *shape* and exerts exactly zero force on magnitude, while $\mathcal L_{PA}$ drives magnitude to zero. The method does not instantiate the mechanism it cites.

**(c) The gradient is not bounded.** The frozen text's boundedness claim covers $G$ ($\|G\|\le1/(d\kappa)$ ✓) but not the $\frac{d}{s}$ prefactor, which diverges as $s=\operatorname{tr}\tilde\Sigma\to0$. For $\ell_2$-normalized embeddings, $\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_w=\frac{1}{B-C_b}\sum_c|I_c|(1-\|\bar z_c\|^2)$; at a typical converged PA spread this is $\approx0.13$, so $d/s\approx3.9\times10^3$ already, and it **grows** as the embedding compacts. Combined with (b): the term is simultaneously indifferent to, and destabilized by, the collapse it is named for. The effective weight of $\mu\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}$ silently rises by an order of magnitude over training. $s=0$ is also reachable in fp16; no $\epsilon$-floor is specified.

**(d) The EMA gradient path is attenuated 20×.** Only $m\Sigma_w^{(t)}$ carries gradient, so $\mu_{\text{eff}}=0.05\mu$. Absorbable into $\mu$ — but $\mu$ is never assigned a value, and (c) means the correct $\mu$ is time-varying.

**(e) The gradient cannot enter unoccupied directions at first order.** $\partial\mathcal L/\partial z_i\propto M(z_i-\bar z_c)$ with $M$ diagonal in the EMA eigenbasis. If the residual has no component on a starved direction $v_j$, the update has none either. The term rebalances variance *within* the currently occupied $\le96$-dim subspace; expansion into new directions is second-order/cross-batch only.

**(f) The revival target is itself the degenerate regime.** Solving $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}=0.45$ for a flat-on-support spectrum gives support fraction $f\approx0.76$ — **~389 of 512 within-class directions carrying equal variance**, on CUB's ~5.9k training images. The only source of ~389 equal-variance within-class directions at that scale is augmentation and nuisance. The frozen text's own falsification criterion presupposes the outcome its primary kill predicts.

**(g) Estimator degrades exactly where the theorem is vacuous.** On SOP/In-Shop with ~2 images per class, $C_b\approx B/2$, per-batch rank $\le64$, per-batch floor $(512-64)/512=0.875$, and each class contributes a rank-1 term. Both the certificate and the estimator fail on the same two datasets.

## 4. Novelty: the exact mechanism is occupied, and its published effect size is ~+1

Searched for the mechanism, not the names.

**Roth et al., ICML 2020** define the spectral-decay metric $\rho(\Phi)=\mathrm{KL}(\mathcal U_D\,\|\,S_{\Phi\mathcal X})$ — KL between the **normalized singular-value spectrum and uniform** — and hypothesize that retaining more directions of significant variance prevents compression that discards non-class-discriminative features, improving DML generalization **to unseen test classes**. That is CCRB's motivating claim verbatim, on this exact benchmark suite, six years prior. CCRB's $\frac{\kappa}{d}\sum(\hat\lambda_i+\kappa)^{-1}$ is a different scalarization of the same trace-normalized flatness functional, minimized at the same uniform spectrum. The genuine delta is that Roth induces flatness by a sampling heuristic ($p_{\text{switch}}$ swap) rather than an explicit loss, and does not condition on class in the barrier. Reported gains, R50/128-D on Margin: CUB 63.09→64.93, Cars 79.86→82.37, **SOP 78.43→78.52** — i.e. nothing on SOP.

**Anti-Collapse Loss (arXiv 2407.03106)** directly maximizes a log-det coding-rate rank surrogate as a DML loss against embedding-space collapse, combined with ProxyAnchor, at **R50/512-D — the exact comparison lane**: CUB 70.6→**71.7**, Cars 89.6→**90.5**, SOP 80.4→**81.2**. It operates on proxies rather than within-class covariance, so CCRB is not identical; but "log-det/coding-rate rank barrier bolted onto ProxyAnchor for DML generalization" is an occupied slot with a measured effect size of **+0.8 to +1.1**.

Add **MCR²** (class-conditional log-det on within-class features, with the opposite sign), **VICReg/VCReg** and **W-MSE** (variance/covariance and whitening barriers), and the frozen author's own citations. The residual novelty is: a matrix barrier instead of a diagonal hinge, class-conditioned, trace-normalized, plus a rank-counting argument that is false (§2). The frozen text calls its own novelty kill "the least hardened of the three"; on this evidence it is the best-supported of the three.

Also directly adverse to the premise: **RankMe** (Garrido et al., ICML 2023) establishes rank↔downstream only *correlationally across hyperparameters*, and **LiDAR** (Thilak et al., ICLR 2024) exists specifically because raw rank is content-agnostic — it replaces rank with the rank of an LDA matrix, weighting directions by discriminability. The published state of this question is that rank alone is known to be an inadequate proxy.

## 5. Controls and the empirical bar

**Controls do not identify content selection.** No control isolates $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}$ from a plain **backbone-plasticity brake**: ImageNet-1K GAP features already have a high-rank, heavy-tailed within-class spectrum, so at initialization the barrier is near its achievable range and mostly penalizes departure from init. Its measurable effect would be confounded with lower backbone LR, stronger weight decay toward init, or partial freezing — all known-strong DML interventions. No such arm is proposed. Nor is there any control separating the contribution of $\mathcal L_{\text{inv}}$ (an SSL invariance term that is itself a known standalone DML gain) from $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}$.

## 6. Cost and protocol

- **Barrier FLOPs are understated ~5×.** Cholesky is $d^3/3\approx4.5\times10^7$ ✓, but the loss needs $\operatorname{tr}(\cdot)^{-1}$ (triangular inverse, $+d^3/3$) and the gradient needs $(\hat\Sigma+\kappa I)^{-2}$ ($+2d^3$): total $\approx3\times10^8$, plus $O(Bd^2)=3.4\times10^7$ to form $\Sigma_w$. Against $\sim5\times10^{11}$ forward FLOPs the "free" conclusion survives; in wall-clock these are latency-bound small dense ops, realistically 1–2% of step time, not zero. EMA state is 1 MB. Fine.
- **1.25× epoch time is the real cost and is not budget-matched** to the 200-epoch PFML reference. "≈1.02× memory" is not derivable — a $\rho=0.25$ micro-batch run sequentially is ~1.0×, run concurrently ~1.25×. Fabricated precision.
- **Inference cost is genuinely zero.** One model, one view, one 512-D descriptor, cosine. Compliant.
- **$\beta$ and $\mu$ are never assigned values.** These two coefficients set the equilibrium of a directly opposed pair — $\mathcal L_{\text{inv}}$ removes augmentation-induced within-class variance, $\mathcal L_{\text{rank}}$ rewards it — so the method's behavior is unspecified. Compounded by (c): the correct $\mu$ drifts during training.
- No epoch budget, seed count, sampler spec, or $\epsilon$-floor for $\operatorname{tr}\tilde\Sigma$. Classes with $|I_c|=1$ contribute zero numerator against an unadjusted $B-C_b$ denominator (minor bias).
- The proposed revival ablation is analysis-only and uses official CUB-2011 segmentations, so it does not violate the no-external-data rule — but it is outside the frozen protocol and does not address the plasticity confound.

## 7. Classification

**DEAD, with substantive repair required.** Two independent defects each suffice:

1. **The rank-exclusion theorem is false for non-affine readouts**, and its global minimum is attainable by a nonlinear re-embedding of the train-class posterior carrying zero additional information about $x$. Restricting the hypothesis to affine $g$ makes it true and nearly vacuous. The frozen text's "one genuinely good part" and its stated generalizable residue both fail.
2. **Trace normalization makes the objective a functional of spectral shape only**, so its global minimum coincides with complete within-class variability collapse — the condition the method was designed to prevent — while its gradient magnitude diverges as $1/\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_w$ in exactly that regime.

Either repair produces a different method, so I do not propose one. The empirical bar fails independently: zero crossings of the corrected same-lane reference on any dataset, against a required two, with the closest published instances of the same mechanism landing at +0.8 to +1.1 in this exact lane.
