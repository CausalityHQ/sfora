# Cross-field candidate batch 205--210

Date: 2026-08-02. Generated with Claude after candidates 203--204 closed the
post-RS@k batch-design route. Primary-neighbour search preceded every verdict.
No diagnostic, implementation, preregistered arm, or GPU work followed.

## Verdict

**NONE.** Six mechanisms were deliberately imported from distinct fields. Each
had numerical repository provenance, but none retained a mechanism-level
novelty distinction after algebra and prior art.

### 205. Slepian--Wolf conditional-rate supervision

Provenance: **17.94%** of same-class epoch-10 In-Shop tangent-gradient pairs
disagreed, and **46.08%** lay below cosine 0.5. The proposal made each image's
code reconstructible from another same-class code through a conditional prior.
It reduces to Multi-View Information Bottleneck when the mate is a view; across
exchangeable same-class mates it becomes class-conditional compression. A
selected mate adds positive mining, and a proxy-response decoder adds the
Classemes/Mahalanobis representation rejected as candidate 201. **DEAD at Gate
2.**

### 206. Rasch specific-objectivity supervision

Provenance: the replicated image-by-image interaction accounted for **4.75%**
of CUB similarity variance. For same-class images `i,j` and foreign proxies
`a,b`, impose
`(s_ia - s_ib) - (s_ja - s_jb) = 0`. This is exactly the double-centred
image-by-proxy tetrad object transferred by TIRD, with its sign turned into an
invariance penalty. TIRD scored **0.8301** against **0.9024** paired Proxy
Anchor on In-Shop (**-7.237 points**). It is also a regularizer over an existing
relation, not new supervision. **DEAD algebraically and empirically.**

### 207. Proximal double-negative-control identity supervision

Provenance: same-acquisition-group cosine rose from a one-step **0.0251** gap to
an epoch-10 **0.1804** gap (**7.18x** amplification). Proximal causal learning
requires both negative-control exposure and outcome variables satisfying
exclusion conditions. Pixels plus identity labels contain no identified pair;
the filename series token is dataset-specific and exact series matching already
failed to explain the association. Executable relaxations are nuisance
projection or environment-invariant similarity, both occupied. **DEAD at Gate
1 and Gate 2.**

### 208. Good--Turing unseen-support reservation

Provenance: In-Shop has 12 singleton and 10 doubleton identities. Good--Turing
unseen mass is only `12/3997 = 0.30%`; Chao richness adds about 7.2 identities,
**0.18%** of the inventory. Both are at or below the already insufficient
**0.253%** ensemble all-miss rescue event. Reserved/virtual proxies are also
Proxy Synthesis and open-set placeholder learning. **DEAD at Gate 1 and Gate
2.**

### 209. Fractional-factorial augmentation contrasts

Provenance: ARCG's In-Shop gate had density **0.363--0.364**, rejected **53.37%**
of close-quartile pairs, and accepted **28.00%** of far-quartile pairs. A
resolution-IV design would estimate unaliased embedding responses to multiple
augmentation factors and equate main-effect vectors across same-class images.
Those vectors are a lower-variance estimator of the same displacement relation
as AugSelf/ARCG; vector equality is occupied by difference-vector and
class--pose methods, and two-factor contrasts are augmentation commutators.
Five factors require about 16 views, violating the ~1x cost bound. **DEAD at
Gate 2 and on cost.**

### 210. Binding-error composite supervision

Provenance: position-tolerant regional MaxSim recovered **+6.67 points** over
fixed slot matching, suggesting spatially unbound identity evidence. The
proposal spatially combined images from different identities and labelled the
composite as neither parent. Mixed-parent targets are Metrix/CutMix; a new
virtual identity is Proxy Synthesis; repelling the composite from both parents
is synthesis plus uniformity, whose in-repo arms were neutral or negative. The
only trainable region arm lost **3.6 points**. **DEAD at Gate 2.**

## Reusable reductions

Rasch-specific objectivity, differential item functioning and conjoint
additivity all land on the same double-centred image-by-proxy interaction that
TIRD already tested. More broadly, the latest cross-field imports fail on
identifiability: the permitted data do not contain the negative control,
semantic factor, or independently observed relation the imported mechanism
requires. Renaming the missing variable does not create supervision.

The search reopens through an independently observed cross-image relation that
survives outcome checks, or through a primary-source audit showing that an
apparently occupied mechanism is materially different. Another unconstrained
analogy batch is not justified by current evidence.
