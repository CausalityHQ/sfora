## Verdict: **DEAD**

**Earliest failed gate: 1** (executable-object gradient and degeneracy audit).

**Decisive mechanism-level reason.** The pooled-shape path — the sole path the proposal claims has "no analogue in any pair-weighting, proxy, or margin loss" — carries the *wrong sign on most of the band it acts on, with larger magnitude than the honest path.* Reproducing the derivatives exactly as specified (λ detached inside the normalization, so α̂₀ ≡ 1 by construction and D = α̂₀ − 2α̂₁ = 1/(2−ξ)):

- ξ-path on one exceedance: `∂L/∂ξ̃ · ∂ξ̂/∂ê_(j) / λᵢ = p̄ ∂_ξg · (2/ND²)(α̂₁ − t)`, `t = (N−j)/(N−1)`
- λ-path on the same element: `(pᵢ/B)(g/r) = p̄ g/N` — the λ factors and the identity **N = B·r** cancel exactly.

Ratio at the bottom of the band: **∂_ξg·(2−ξ)(3−ξ)/g**, which is **2.5 at the ξ = −3 clamp, 6.0 at ξ = −1, 21 at ξ = 0, 21 at ξ = 0.4** (SOP; 2.5–14 on CUB). It never falls below 1 anywhere in the admissible range. The net field pushes a negative *down* only for `t ≤ α̂₁ + gD²/(2∂_ξg)`, i.e. **the top 28.5 % of pooled exceedances at ξ = 0, top 64 % at the clamp**. So **36–72 % of all top-band negatives receive a net gradient that raises their similarity**, maximal at the very elements nearest the threshold, on every dataset.

Following that field: raising the low exceedances sends ê → 1, so D → 0 and ξ̂ → −∞ (clamped, gradient zeroed), and g collapses 9.10 → 1.33. Concretely at SOP with u = 0.40, exponential excesses of mean λ = 0.05: honest ŝ⁻ = 0.855; band flattened up to its own max (E[max] = λH₂₆ = 0.192) gives ŝ⁻ ≈ 0.74 — **the loss drops ≈ 0.11 cosine while every one of the 26–27 top negatives has risen and the mean top-band similarity goes 0.45 → 0.68.** It pays on all four datasets because H_r = 3.83 < g(0)/g(−3) = 5.1 (CUB) … 6.8 (SOP).

This is exactly the shortcut §2.4 was built to remove, reintroduced with 2.5–21× the magnitude through the advertised novelty. §2.2's flattened-band defense fails because it only considered flattening *down* to u (λ → ε); the gradient prefers flattening *up*, where λ grows 3.8× but g shrinks 6.8×. And the terminal state is a dichotomy with no good branch: either the estimator is being gamed, or ξ̄ parks at the hard clamp where path 4's gradient is identically zero and TERL **is** control C3 with c = 4/3 — a *smaller* multiplier than the honest ln x it was supposed to supply.

## Gate-by-gate

**1 — FAILED (stability, not correctness).** Formulas verified correct (below). But `D ≥ 0` with equality iff all normalized exceedances are tied (co-monotone weights, Σc_j = 0), `ξ̂ = 2 − α̂₀/D` ⇒ `D = 1/(2−ξ)`, so the clamp `[−3, 0.4]` is a clamp on `D ∈ [0.20, 0.625]`, and `∂ξ̂/∂ê ∝ 1/D²` is unbounded as ties form. Descent drives the sample *toward* that boundary (above). The object is well-defined a.e. only because a hard clamp truncates it, and the clamp's dead zone sits at the optimizer's destination. Also: `r = ⌈0.15·176⌉ = ⌈26.4⌉ = 27`, not 26; `N = 4,860`, not 4,680 — r propagates into the warm-up schedule, C8's regressor, and N.

**2 — FAILED.** At exact total collapse the descriptor gradient is **exactly zero at first order**, contra "not stationary." For any loss that is a function of the Gram matrix of unit vectors, `dS_ij = ẑ_j·dẑ_i + ẑ_i·dẑ_j = 0` when all ẑ are equal and `dẑ ⊥ ẑ`; every path (positive pull, u, λ, and the α̂₀ < 10⁻⁵ guard) vanishes. Positive loss ≠ non-stationary. This is generic to cosine losses, so it does not discriminate TERL, but the claimed structural defense is false. Also `s̃⁺ = 1 + ln3/20 = 1.055`, not 1. The cap bound is valid but only establishes a floor for small θ; "compression is strictly penalized" overstates a decreasing lower bound. Scale/temperature absence and the wd/spherical-dynamics caveat are correct.

**3 — FAILED.** §2.4's algebra is right (`w_k(1−g) + (g/r)1{k≤r}`, `1−g < 0`) and the diagnosis is right, but the repair is incomplete: the stated path-4 derivative is correct in form yet directionally wrong in description ("positive only for the largest exceedances … rare catastrophic collisions"). The true crossover is at `α̂₁/α̂₀ = (1−D)/2 = 0.25` at ξ = 0 — the positive region is the top quartile (~1,200 pooled values), and the B-fold accumulation of the shared ξ̃ across all 180 sites (unstated) is what makes the negative region dominate. The backward is not the gradient of any scalar, so there is no Lyapunov function; the constructed gaming update above is the cheapest descent direction. `no-sg` and ρ = 1 do not ablate it; only C2 (freezing ξ) does — and C2 should therefore *beat* full TERL.

**4 — FAILED.** The error mode is an existence claim about the GPD family ("two encoders can differ by 0.25"), not a measurement. Nothing prospectively measures fitted ξ on a trained encoder, its seed/epoch stability, its transport to disjoint identities, or its incremental prediction of official-query errors beyond u and λ. Balkema–de Haan/Pickands is asymptotic and silent on a 344× extrapolation from 26 dependent exceedances of a suspected mixture. C9 compares ŝ⁻ against the realized full-training-set max — in-sample, same-identity, and against the statistic §2.1 itself calls "the wrong transfer statistic," so F4 can pass while the disjoint-identity extrapolation is wrong. What would be required: (i) per-query (u, λ, ξ) fitted on a frozen encoder for train vs held-out identities, with distributional equality and per-query correlation across the split; (ii) nested regression showing ξ adds R² for query-level R@1 error beyond u, λ, and batch max; (iii) an intervention matching u and λ across encoders and varying ξ alone.

**5 — FAILED (novelty claim false as stated).** §3's "Web search returned no train-time GPD/return-level objective in DML or retrieval" is wrong on the GPD half. **WEINCE** (arXiv 2606.00262, May 2026; ICML 2026) applies POT to in-batch negative cosine similarities, fits a per-anchor tail to the near-endpoint shortfalls, and interpolates softmax with shortfall logits inside the training loss — and **deliberately stop-gradients the tail index and mixing weight ("no backprop through the tail fits")**. **LDReg** (ICLR 2024) already differentiates through a tail-index-family (LID) estimator of a learned distance distribution as a training objective. So the supervision action "fit a negative-score tail in-batch at train time and reshape the loss by it" is prior art; TERL's residual novelty reduces to (a) backprop through the shape and (b) return-level extrapolation to M. The nearest prior work chose *against* (a) explicitly, and gate 1 shows why. (TriSim: not located; does not affect the verdict.) The rest of §3's distinctions (EVM/OpenMax/meta-recognition post-hoc; DeepGPD supervised; EVL reweighting; SPOT fixed signal; index-flood imported) are sound in kind.

**6 — FAILED; not reversed.** Pooling (N ≈ 4,860 across 180 sites) + EMA attacks *variance*, and that is a legitimate improvement over per-query fitting. It cannot manufacture the missing signal: incremental R² of 0.0087 for shape beyond threshold and mean excess is a statement that path 4 carries almost no information about deployment depth; shape noise at 67–86 % of the depth signal means either genuine cross-site heterogeneity (index-flood pooling is then *biased*, not merely smoothed) or that shape is uninformative. Decisively, deep-quantile RMSE constant 0.0918 vs fitted 0.1575 says the **constant-multiple-of-mean-excess form beats the fitted form by 1.7×** — and `u + (4/3)λ` at the clamp *is* that constant form, i.e. control C3. The supplied evidence predicts C3 ≥ TERL, and F2's response to that is "report it as a fixed margin," not "dead."

**7 — arithmetic errors, and no identification.** `ln(Mζ/r)` for In-Shop is **5.01**, not 4.60 — 4.60 = ln(25882·0.10/26), i.e. computed at ζ = 0.10. Corrected regressor {3.52, 3.84, 5.01, 5.84} against forecast Δ {2.2, 2.3, 2.0, 3.0} gives slope **+0.25 pt/log-unit, t = 1.12, df = 2, 90 % CI [−0.41, +0.91]** — positive point estimate with CI covering 0, which per F3's literal wording is **not** a falsification. F3 cannot fire on the author's own forecast: it is a non-test. With n = 4, `ln(Mζ/r) = ln M + const` is collinear with training-set size, class count (100/98/3997/11318) and mean class size (59/82/6.5/5.3); gallery depth is unidentified. M is the *training* image count while the deployment event is over a 10⁴–10⁵ test gallery, so the regressor is literally ln(train-set size). §5's frontier z's use SDs where F5 uses SEs (a √5 discrepancy); conclusions unchanged. Verified correct: 9.097/4.05/Δ = 0.253; 343.6×; 34×; the 28-seeds power figure.

**8 — controls under-specified where it matters.** C1/C3 are well-designed (C3's magnitude matching is the right construction). But C4 ("best taken" over α ∈ {8,16,32,64}) and C6 ("best-of-sweep per arm" over 3 wd values) never state the *selection criterion*; §1.9's class-disjoint rule covers only ζ and m. Either those arms are selected on test R@1 — a legal-envelope violation of "no test-time fitting in selection," contradicting "test data is touched exactly once" — or the protocol is incomplete. No raw-vs-final distinction appears anywhere. Power: F1's 0.5 pt on SOP is ≈ 2.3 SE at 5 seeds; F2's 0.4 pt is ≈ 1.8 SE — the two decisive-triad thresholds are one-shot and under-powered, and F2/F3 outcomes are reclassifications, not kills.

**9 — recipe/cost mostly matched, screen order not.** FLOP-ratio 1.4e-7 and 130 KB check out; F6 guards wall-clock. Backbone BN trainability is never stated (only "no BN in the head" and one frozen epoch) — an unmatched degree of freedom against PA references. The required protocol — paired corrected In-Shop screened **first**, raw plus independently-selected/final, out-of-sample confirmation, replication — is absent: §5 runs all four datasets, reports one number per arm, and benchmarks In-Shop against PA+DADA 0.930 rather than the corrected paired local reference (raw 0.9163/0.9189, final 0.9137/0.9168, seed 2 pending).

**10 — no standing-objective value.** The proposal itself forecasts no crossing anywhere: CUB −1.8 (z = −2.68 on its own conservative convention, −6.0 on F5's), Cars −2.8, SOP −0.1 (tie), In-Shop +0.3 nominal with σ_ref unreported and F5 barring the claim. The entire +2.2…+3.0 is measured against its own reproduction, which sits 4.0/5.1/3.1 pt below the audited PFML frontier. Per the standing rule, a nominal mean inside uncertainty and a self-reproduced weak baseline earn no credit — so the only asset was the mechanism claim, which gate 1 removes.

## Preserved correct subcomponents (independent of the verdict)

1. **Hosking–Wallis PWM → GPD shape conversion is exactly right.** `α_r = σ/((r+1)(r+1−ξ))` ⇒ `α₀/(α₀−2α₁) = 2−ξ` ⇒ `ξ̂ = 2 − α̂₀/(α̂₀−2α̂₁)`; the ascending-order r = 1 plotting position `(N−j)/(N−1)` is the correct unbiased estimator of `E[Y(1−F(Y))]`; exponential → 0 and uniform → −1 both check.
2. **Return-level algebra is right.** `σ = λ(1−ξ)`, `u + λ(1−ξ)(x^ξ−1)/ξ` is the standard POT (1−1/x) level; `∂_ξ g > 0` verified at ξ = −3, −1, −0.3, 0, 0.4; `g → 1`, `g(−1) ≈ 2`, `g(0) = ln x`; hence `ŝ⁻ ≥ u + λ` and no sub-threshold extrapolation is representable. The expm1/series implementation note is correct.
3. **§2.4's own derivation and sign diagnosis** (`w_k(1−g) + (g/r)1{k≤r}`) are correct — the failure is that the same pathology recurs through ξ, not that this analysis is wrong.
4. **Index-flood pooling + straight-through EMA** is a legitimate variance-reduction construction for a shape estimator; it is the one part that responds to the prior tail-diagnostic evidence, and it should be retained by any future work on shape estimation over batches.
5. **C1, C3 (magnitude-matched), C9 (training-set-only calibration), F5's uncertainty prohibition, and the 28-seed power calculation** are all sound instruments, as is the frozen no-crossing conclusion and the honest list of source ambiguities.

## Uncertainty

The gate-1 result is analytic and rests only on the frozen equations plus α̂₀ ≡ 1 (forced by the index-flood normalization with detached λ); the ratio and sign-crossover formulas are exact for a GPD-distributed normalized sample. What I cannot settle without runs: the realized trajectory of ξ̄ under the EMA and warm-up (my analysis fixes the direction of the field at every ξ, not the dwell time at each), and whether the clamp is implemented as a gradient-zeroing hard clamp — but both branches of that dichotomy are fatal to path 4. Gate 5's WEINCE reading comes from the arXiv HTML, not the camera-ready; the LDReg characterization is from the abstract/OpenReview record plus the standard Hill-family construction of LID estimators, so treat it as strong-but-secondary. TriSim I could not locate. Every finding above is adjudication only; any repair — retaining the pooled PWM estimator while removing the non-conservative shape gradient, or replacing return-level extrapolation with the constant form the supplied evidence favours — is a new proposal, not a fix to this one.
