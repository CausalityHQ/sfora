# In-Shop asymmetric TRAIN-only proxy check, 26 September 2026

The current unseen-product TRAIN gallery scores all 12,599 held images as symmetric queries and gallery rows with self excluded. The official TEST protocol has 14,218 queries and 12,612 gallery images over 3,985 products. Its gallery averages 3.165 images per product, versus 6.322 images per product in the symmetric held gallery. This mismatch may saturate top-1 and distort training-arm selection. It does not by itself explain the official quality gap.

Before any new quality training arm, re-export the **existing** seed-179024 full-control, old-freeze and true-freeze checkpoints on the same held 12,599 official-TRAIN images. Require each exported 128-D int8 plus fp16-norm packed vector set to reproduce its original symmetric per-query Recall@1 and mAP@R **exactly**; otherwise stop and repair checkpoint/export parity. Partition every held product independently: order its images by SHA-256 of the relative image path, then assign `round(n/2)` images to gallery, bounded to `[1,n−1]`, and the rest to query. The resulting membership is fixed by metadata only; no model output affects it. It yields 6,245 gallery rows and 6,354 queries over 1,993 unseen products, or 3.133 gallery images per product. All query products have at least one gallery positive. Score full rectangular packed cosine with stable ordinal ties; report Recall@1, mAP@R, per-query vectors, paired product-bootstrap contrasts and export/scoring wall and CUDA memory on DGX Spark GB10.

Use this only as a **proxy diagnostic**, never as an official read or a retroactive rescue of a failed frozen gate. It may inform a *future* predeclared selection protocol if the full-control miss rate is at least twice its symmetric held miss rate and the arm ordering is sufficiently stable to motivate independent-seed confirmation. A weaker enrichment or inconsistent arm ordering rejects this particular asymmetric instrument. Even a positive result cannot establish official transfer: it retains only 1,993 held products, about half the official product count, and the fit model saw only half of official TRAIN products. No fresh training or official TEST scoring follows solely from this readout.
