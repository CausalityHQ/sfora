I did an adversarial primary-source search before writing. Two findings changed the design: PFML's exact reduction and recipe are recoverable (so I can extend it verbatim), and **Spherical Feature Transform (ECCV 2020)** occupies part of the geometric ground I was heading for, which forced the mechanism onto a different axis (parameter-capacity rationing rather than variance transfer).

# PROPOSAL — GVF: Generic Variation Frame

**One method. Lane A (matched 512-D ResNet-50 CNN lane) for every forecast and comparison.**

GVF changes exactly one object in PFML: the *parameterization of the per-class proxy set*. PFML gives every class M free proxies. GVF gives every class one free mean plus a **single rank-K orthonormal frame of tangent directions shared by all classes**, from which each class's M proxies are generated by geodesic displacement. The same frame is re-anchored at individual sample embeddings to build single-image virtual classes, which is the train-time replica of the deployment operation. The frame is annealed toward the deployment metric and discarded at test time.

---

## 0. The reduction being extended, reproduced exactly as disclosed

PFML (Potential Field Based Deep Metric Learning, arXiv:2405.18560, accepted CVPR 2025), verbatim:

$$\psi_{\text{att}}(r,z)=\begin{cases}-\delta^{-\alpha} & \|r-z\|_2<\delta\\ -\|r-z\|_2^{-\alpha} & \text{else}\end{cases}\qquad
\psi_{\text{rep}}(r,z)=\begin{cases}\|r-z\|_2^{-\alpha} & \|r-z\|_2<\delta\\ \delta^{-\alpha} & \text{else}\end{cases}$$

$$\Psi_j(r)=\underbrace{\sum_{i:y_i=j}\psi_{\text{att}}(r,z_i)+\sum_{k=1}^{M}\psi_{\text{att}}(r,p_{j,k})}_{\Psi_{j,\text{att}}}+\underbrace{\sum_{i:y_i\neq j}\psi_{\text{rep}}(r,z_i)+\sum_{\gamma\neq j}\sum_k \psi_{\text{rep}}(r,p_{\gamma,k})}_{\Psi_{j,\text{rep}}}$$

$$U_{\text{PFML}}=\sum_{i=1}^{|B|}\Psi_{y_i}(z_i)\;+\;\sum_{j=1}^{N}\sum_{k=1}^{M}\Psi_j(p_{j,k})$$

Disclosed recipe: ResNet-50 with BN, 512-D $\ell_2$-normalized embedding, 224×224 train crops, test = center crop from 256×256, Adam, backbone lr 5e-4, **proxy lr = 5e-4 × 100**, 200 epochs on each dataset, $\alpha$ cross-validated in $\{0,\dots,6\}$, $\delta\in[0.1,0.3]$, $M=15$ (CUB), $15$ (Cars), $2$ (SOP). Reported: CUB $73.4\pm0.3$, Cars $92.7\pm0.3$, SOP $82.9\pm0.2$, 5 runs. All of this is inherited unchanged.

**Undisclosed in the source (§7 lists these as blocking ambiguities): batch size/sampler composition, LR schedule, weight decay, augmentation, per-dataset $\alpha$ and $\delta$, proxy initialization, whether proxies are re-projected to the sphere, GAP vs GAP+GMP head, BN freezing, and whether R@1 is best-epoch or last-epoch.** No frontier is inherited: every GVF number is reported against an in-house PFML reproduction run in the same code with the same seeds.

## 1. Executable mathematics

### 1.1 Learned objects

| symbol | shape | role | weight decay | lr |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\psi$ | ResNet-50 | backbone, ImageNet-1K init | 1e-4 | 5e-4 |
| $W$ | $512\times2048$ | embedding, no bias | 1e-4 | 5e-4 |
| $\mu_j$ | $512$, $j=1..N$ | class means (proxy anchors) | **0** | 5e-2 |
| $\Omega$ | $512\times K$ | **shared frame generator** | **0** | 5e-2 |
| $\tau_k$ | $K$ | **shared amplitude logits** | **0** | 5e-2 |

Nothing else. $|\Omega|+|\tau|=Kd+K$ parameters describe *all* intra-class structure for *all* classes.

$$z_i=\frac{W\,\mathrm{GAP}(\psi(x_i))}{\|W\,\mathrm{GAP}(\psi(x_i))\|_2}\in S^{511},\qquad \hat\mu_j=\frac{\mu_j}{\|\mu_j\|_2}$$

### 1.2 The frame

Thin QR with positive-diagonal $R$ (makes $Q$ unique, hence the gradient well-defined):

$$Q=\mathrm{qr}(\Omega).Q=[\hat w_1,\dots,\hat w_K],\qquad \langle \hat w_k,\hat w_l\rangle=\delta_{kl}\ \text{ identically}$$

Amplitudes, bounded and strictly positive:

$$\theta_k=\theta_{\max}\,\sigma(\tau_k),\qquad \theta_{\max}=\pi/4,\qquad \tau_k(0)=\mathrm{logit}(0.2/\theta_{\max})=-1.075\ \ (\theta_k(0)=0.2\,\text{rad})$$

### 1.3 Generated proxy set (replaces PFML's free $p_{j,k}$)

Tangent projection at the class mean, then exact geodesic displacement:

$$t_k(j)=(I-\hat\mu_j\hat\mu_j^{\!\top})\hat w_k,\qquad g_k(j)=\|t_k(j)\|_2\in[0,1],\qquad n_k(j)=t_k(j)/\max(g_k(j),\epsilon),\ \epsilon=10^{-6}$$

$$\boxed{\;p_{j,0}=\hat\mu_j,\qquad p_{j,k,\pm}=\cos\!\big(\beta(t)\,\theta_k\,g_k(j)\big)\,\hat\mu_j\;\pm\;\sin\!\big(\beta(t)\,\theta_k\,g_k(j)\big)\,n_k(j)\;}$$

Every $p$ is exactly on $S^{511}$ (no renormalization). The gate $g_k(j)$ makes the construction **pole-free**: when $\hat\mu_j \parallel \hat w_k$, $g\to0$ and $p_{j,k,\pm}\to\hat\mu_j$ continuously. This is why projection is used rather than canonical parallel transport $\Gamma_{o\to\mu}(v)=v-\frac{\langle\mu,v\rangle}{1+\langle o,\mu\rangle}(o+\mu)$, whose $1/(1+\langle o,\mu\rangle)$ blows up for the classes that inevitably land near $-o$ (transport is run as ablation C4b).

Proxy count: $M=2K+1$. **$K=7\Rightarrow M=15$, exactly PFML's CUB/Cars setting.** For SOP, unsigned $K=1$ gives $M=2$, exactly PFML's SOP setting. Matched by construction.

### 1.4 Locus 2 — single-image virtual classes

For each sample $i$, anchor the *same* frame at its own embedding, with a stop-gradient on the anchor:

$$\bar z_i=\mathrm{sg}(z_i),\quad t_k(i)=(I-\bar z_i\bar z_i^{\!\top})\hat w_k,\quad v_{i,0}=\bar z_i,\quad v_{i,k,\pm}=\cos(\beta\theta_k g_k(i))\bar z_i\pm\sin(\beta\theta_k g_k(i))n_k(i)$$

$$U_{\text{1shot}}=\frac{1}{|B|}\sum_{i}\Big[\tfrac{1}{|P_i|}\sum_{i'\in P_i}\sum_{v\in V_i}\psi_{\text{att}}(z_{i'},v)\;+\;\tfrac{1}{|N_i|}\sum_{i''\in N_i}\sum_{v\in V_i}\psi_{\text{rep}}(z_{i''},v)\Big]$$

$P_i=\{i'\neq i: y_{i'}=y_i\}$, $N_i=\{i'': y_{i''}\neq y_i\}$, same $\psi,\alpha,\delta$. This asks literally the deployment question — *given one image and a generic variation model, retrieve the rest of its class against everything else* — with **zero class-indexed parameters**.

### 1.5 Total objective and schedule

$$\boxed{\;U=U_{\text{PFML}}\big(\{p_{j,k}\}\ \text{generated by §1.3}\big)\;+\;\eta\,U_{\text{1shot}},\qquad \eta=0.5\;}$$

$$\beta(t)=\max\!\Big(0.25,\;1-\tfrac{t}{T}\Big),\quad T=200\ \text{epochs (no gradient through }\beta)$$

Annealing closes the train/deploy metric gap: training ends with proxy manifolds at a quarter radius, close to the plain-cosine geometry that is actually deployed.

### 1.6 Gradient paths (complete)

- $\partial U/\partial\Omega$: through the differentiable QR into $\hat w_k$, into $t_k(j),g_k(j),n_k(j)$ for all $j$, and into $t_k(i),n_k(i)$ for all $i$ in Locus 2.
- $\partial U/\partial\tau_k$: through $\theta_k=\theta_{\max}\sigma(\tau_k)$ into the $\cos/\sin$ arguments at both loci.
- $\partial U/\partial\mu_j$: through $\hat\mu_j$ into $p_{j,\cdot}$, from both PFML terms ($\sum_i\Psi_{y_i}(z_i)$ and $\sum_j\sum_k\Psi_j(p_{j,k})$).
- $\partial U/\partial(\psi,W)$: through $z_i$ in $U_{\text{PFML}}$; in $U_{\text{1shot}}$ **only** through $z_{i'},z_{i''}$ — never through the anchor.
- After each optimizer step: $\mu_j\leftarrow\mu_j/\|\mu_j\|$ (fixes the effective angular step size under Adam; see §6).

### 1.7 Train / test operations

**Train:** class-balanced sampler, 32 classes × 4 images = 128; RandomResizedCrop(224, scale=(0.16,1)) + horizontal flip; Adam, cosine LR decay with 5-epoch linear warmup, 200 epochs; $\alpha,\delta$ from PFML's cross-validated grid, re-cross-validated on the class-holdout split (§7).

**Test:** resize 256 → center crop 224 → one forward → $z=W\mathrm{GAP}(\psi(x))/\|\cdot\|$ → cosine NN. $\mu,\Omega,\tau$ are **deleted**. Single model, one view, 512-D, ordinary cosine retrieval. Lane A compliant.

### 1.8 Pseudocode

```
Q = qr(Omega).Q ; th = th_max * sigmoid(tau) ; b = max(0.25, 1 - t/T)
mu_h = normalize(mu)                                   # [N,512]
t_k  = Q[None] - mu_h[:,:,None] * (mu_h @ Q)[:,None]   # [N,512,K] tangent
g    = norm(t_k, dim=1); n = t_k / clamp(g, min=1e-6)
a    = b * th[None] * g                                # [N,K] geodesic angle
P    = cat([mu_h[:,None], cos(a)[...,None]*mu_h[:,None] + sin(a)[...,None]*n.transpose(),
                          cos(a)[...,None]*mu_h[:,None] - sin(a)[...,None]*n.transpose()], 1)
U    = pfml_energy(Z, Y, P, alpha, delta) + eta * pfml_energy_virtual(Z, Y, frame(sg(Z)), alpha, delta)
```

## 2. One causal zero-shot error mode, and a proof-level attack on the cheapest degeneracies

### 2.1 The error mode: **class-indexed nuisance absorption**

Training loss can be reduced by two routes that are indistinguishable to the objective and opposite in transfer value:

- **R1 (transfers):** the backbone learns image-content functions that suppress nuisance, so the residual field $r(x)=\mathrm{Log}_{\hat\mu_{y(x)}}f(x)$ becomes small and structurally regular. This mechanism is a function of pixels, so it applies verbatim to a class never seen.
- **R2 (does not transfer):** the *proxy set* is enlarged to cover whatever residual field the backbone happens to emit. This costs no representational work and is linear in free parameters.

R2 is cheaper, so gradient descent takes it. Quantitatively, on CUB, PFML's intra-class-structure parameters beyond the class mean number $100\times14\times512=716{,}800$ for **5,864** training images — **122 free parameters per training image**. On Cars: $98\times14\times512=702{,}464$ for 8,054 images = 87/image. The proxy set alone has the capacity to memorize the embedding of every training image. At test time no proxies exist; every unit of R2 progress evaporates, and the novel class manifolds retain the un-normalized residual field.

The failure then materializes through the **max over the gallery**: R@1 fails iff *some* wrong item beats *every* right item. Under a Gaussian residual model with novel-class centre directions unconstrained by training, per-direction confusion probability behaves like $\exp(-\Delta^2/2v)$ in the directional residual variance $v$, which is convex in $v$ for $v<\Delta^2/2$ (the operating regime): $\frac{d^2}{dv^2}e^{-a/v}=\frac{a}{v^3}e^{-a/v}\!\left(\frac{a}{v}-2\right)>0$. By Jensen, at fixed total residual energy, error is minimized when the residual is *spread evenly and shared across classes*, and maximized when it is class-idiosyncratic. R2 produces exactly the class-idiosyncratic case.

AdvRF (ICCV 2025) diagnoses the same disease from the opposite end — "category-specific semantics… creating semantic dependencies on predefined classes that critically hinder generalization to unseen categories" — and treats it with a reconstruction network plus distillation. GVF treats it by **rationing the parameter capacity available to encode class-specific intra-class structure**: $3{,}591$ shared parameters on CUB, i.e. **0.61 per training image, a ~200× reduction**, at essentially zero cost. On SOP the ratio is ~11,000× ($5{,}794{,}816 \to 513$).

### 2.2 Degeneracy D1 — the frame switches itself off ($\theta\to0$)

Let $z=\cos\phi\,\hat\mu_j+\sin\phi\,n$, $a_k=\langle n,n_k(j)\rangle$, $v_0=2-2\cos\phi$, $f(v)=-v^{-\alpha/2}$. Then $\|z-p_{j,k,\pm}\|^2=2-2\cos\phi\cos\theta\mp2\sin\phi\sin\theta\,a_k$. Expanding the **signed pair jointly** (the ± terms are what matter):

$$f(v_0+\theta^2\cos\phi-u)+f(v_0+\theta^2\cos\phi+u)=2f(v_0)+2\theta^2\cos\phi\,f'(v_0)+u^2f''(v_0)+O(\theta^4),\quad u=2\theta\sin\phi\,a_k$$

The **first-order terms cancel exactly** — so $\theta=0$ is a critical point, not a descent direction. (The same cancellation occurs under the SoftTriple soft-assignment reduction, so this is a property of any ±-symmetric construction, not of PFML.) With $f'(v)=\tfrac{\alpha}{2}v^{-\alpha/2-1}>0$ and $f''(v)=-\tfrac{\alpha}{2}(\tfrac{\alpha}{2}+1)v^{-\alpha/2-2}<0$:

$$\Delta U_{\text{att},k}=\theta^2\tfrac{\alpha}{2}v_0^{-\alpha/2-1}\Big[2\cos\phi-\tfrac{4\sin^2\!\phi\,a_k^2(\alpha/2+1)}{v_0}\Big]$$

**Consequences, all operational:**

1. **$\theta=0$ is a strict saddle, not a minimum**, whenever $\Delta U<0$. Substituting $v_0=4\sin^2(\phi/2)$, $\sin^2\phi=4\sin^2(\phi/2)\cos^2(\phi/2)$:
$$a_k^2\;>\;\frac{1-\tfrac{1}{2}\sec^2(\phi/2)}{\alpha/2+1}\;\xrightarrow[\ \phi\ \text{small}\ ]{}\;\frac{1}{\alpha+2}$$
2. **Mandatory nonzero init.** Because the gradient vanishes at $\theta=0$, $\theta_k(0)=0.2$ rad is not a convenience — it is required for the mechanism to exist at all.
3. **A cheap, decisive pre-experiment.** With $\alpha=3$, the frame only pays if a shared rank-$K$ subspace captures $\gtrsim20\%$ of within-class residual *direction* energy. **Experiment 0 (run before anything else):** train PFML verbatim on CUB, take the converged embeddings, compute per-class tangent residuals, project to a common frame, and measure the top-7 shared-subspace explained variance $E_7$. **If $E_7<1/(\alpha+2)$, GVF will not fire and I predict failure.** This is derived, not fitted, and costs one baseline run.

### 2.3 Degeneracy D2 — the frame captures *signal* instead of nuisance

Collecting the $u^2f''$ terms across positives (attraction) and negatives (repulsion), the stationary condition for the frame is

$$\hat w_{1..K}=\text{top-}K\ \text{eigenvectors of}\ \ \underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{\text{pos}}\!\big[\omega_{\text{att}}\,nn^{\!\top}\big]}_{\text{within-class residual}}-\underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{\text{neg}}\!\big[\omega_{\text{rep}}\,n'n'^{\!\top}\big]}_{\text{between-class residual}},\qquad \omega\propto\tfrac{\alpha}{2}\big(\tfrac{\alpha}{2}+1\big)v^{-\alpha/2-2}\sin^2\!\phi$$

A frame aligned with a between-class direction displaces *every* class's proxies toward its neighbours, which increases the repulsion energy at second order with the *opposite* sign. **Signal-aligned frames are maxima of $-U$, not minima.** The mechanism is therefore self-certifying: it cannot converge to the shortcut. This is checked empirically as F3 (§5) — and it is a *falsifier even if R@1 improves*, since a signal-aligned frame would mean the gain came from something other than the claimed mechanism.

### 2.4 Remaining cheap degeneracies, each closed

| # | Degeneracy | Why it is closed |
|---|---|---|
| D3 | Embedding collapse | Inherited from PFML: $\psi_{\text{att}}$ is *constant* inside $\delta$ (zero gradient) and $\psi_{\text{rep}}\to\infty$ as distance $\to0$. Collapse has unbounded energy. |
| D4 | Frame redundancy ($\hat w_k$ all equal) | Impossible by construction: $Q$ from thin QR satisfies $\langle\hat w_k,\hat w_l\rangle=\delta_{kl}$ identically. **No penalty term needed** — contrast SoftTriple, which must add $\tau\sum_{k<k'}\sqrt{2-2w^{k\top}w^{k'}}$ ($\tau=0.2$) precisely because its free centres merge. |
| D5 | Anchor shortcut in Locus 2 | $\mathrm{sg}(z_i)$ removes the "move the anchor onto its positives" path, which would reduce $U_{\text{1shot}}$ to an ordinary pair loss. The only remaining paths are the frame and the *other* samples. |
| D6 | Proxy-set interleaving | With free per-class proxies, two nearby classes' proxy clouds can interpenetrate. Tied displacement moves neighbouring classes' proxies along the *same* directions, keeping the sets parallel. |
| D7 | Train/deploy metric mismatch | $\beta(t)\!\downarrow\!0.25$ ends training near the plain-cosine geometry actually deployed; ablated as C7. |
| D8 | Scale/regularizer cheats | §6 — three non-obvious traps, all real. |

## 3. Adversarial novelty search — nearest works, one-sentence mechanism distinction each

**Inside DML**

1. **PFML (CVPR 2025)** — free per-class proxy sets. *GVF keeps the field, potentials, $\alpha$, $\delta$ and recipe byte-for-byte and replaces $C\!\cdot\!M$ free proxy vectors with $C$ means plus one shared rank-$K$ orthonormal frame.*
2. **SoftTriple (ICCV 2019)**, $\lambda{=}20,\gamma{=}0.1,\delta{=}0.01,\tau{=}0.2$, $K{=}10$ free centres — *GVF's centres are generated, so redundancy is impossible by construction rather than suppressed by a $\tau$-penalty.*
3. **ProxyGML (NeurIPS 2020)** — $M$ free sub-proxies per class plus reverse label propagation on similarity subgraphs. *The sub-proxies remain per-class free parameters; GVF removes exactly that degree of freedom.*
4. **DVML (ECCV 2018)** — the paper that first stated that intra-class variance is "independent on classes", realized as an isotropic-Gaussian VAE latent used to *sample synthetic examples*. *GVF has no generative model, no sampling and no isotropy assumption: it learns a deterministic rank-$K$ orthonormal frame that re-parameterizes the proxy set and is annealed to the deployment metric.*
5. **Spherical Feature Transform (ECCV 2020)** — **the nearest work.** It relaxes identical-covariance to *similar* covariance on the hypersphere and transfers an observed sample's residual to another class by **rotation** rather than translation, to synthesize features. *SFT is nonparametric sample→sample feature augmentation on the loss's sample side; GVF learns an explicit low-rank shared subspace as a parameter that replaces the proxy parameterization, and derives (§2.3) that this parameter converges to the top eigenvectors of within-minus-between residual scatter.* **This is my highest-risk overlap and I could not machine-read the paper's PDF (§7).**
6. **NIR / non-isotropic probabilistic proxy DML (CVPR 2022 / ECCV 2022)** — normalizing flows enforce unique translatability of samples from their class proxy. *NIR shapes the conditional density around each proxy with a learned flow; GVF changes the proxy set itself into a tied low-rank manifold and adds no density model.*
7. **Proxy Synthesis / MemVir / Embedding Expansion** — virtual classes by interpolation or memory. *GVF's single-image virtual class is generated by a learned shared nuisance frame, not by interpolating between existing points, proxies or past steps.*
8. **DiVA / S2SD / Sharing Matters** — auxiliary complementary objectives, self-distillation or shared-feature branches. *GVF adds no branch, teacher, or auxiliary feature space.*
9. **Ranked List Loss** — deliberately preserves intra-class variance via a hinge. *RLL bounds the magnitude of intra-class spread; GVF constrains its direction structure to a shared subspace and says nothing about magnitude.*
10. **PA+DADA (AAAI 2024)** — data-augmented domain adaptation aligning proxy and sample distributions (1.06× epoch time). *DADA adds an adaptation objective between two distributions; GVF re-parameterizes one of them.*
11. **AdvRF (ICCV 2025, Lane B)** — identical diagnosis, treated with a ResNet-34/U-Net reconstruction system plus distillation. *Same disease, opposite treatment: auxiliary generative machinery vs. removing $\sim$99.5% of the parameters that make the disease possible.*
12. **HIST (CVPR 2022) / hierarchical proxies** — hypergraph/hierarchy over classes. *Structure over class means; GVF's structure is over intra-class variation, orthogonal.*

**Outside DML**

13. **ESRC — intraclass variant dictionary (Deng, Hu, Guo, TPAMI 2012)** — the origin of the assumption that "the intraclass variations of one subject can be approximated by a sparse linear combination of those of other subjects", used at **test** time inside a linear sparse-coding classifier. *GVF imports the assumption into training as a differentiable rank-$K$ frame that shapes a deep encoder, and deploys plain cosine with no dictionary — the dictionary is a scaffold, not a scorer.* This is the principled import; the honest lineage runs through classical undersampled face recognition, not DML.
14. **PLDA / WCCN (speaker verification)** — tied within-class covariance in a *backend scorer*, the field's default for zero-shot identity retrieval. *A backend would be test-gallery fitting, which this lane forbids; GVF instead forces the encoder to make the identity the right scorer.*
15. **Tangent Distance / TangentProp (Simard et al.)** — invariance from *known analytic* transformation tangents, one set per example. *GVF learns the tangents from identity labels, shares one frame across all classes, and uses it to generate proxies rather than to define the distance.*
16. **Lie-group transformation learning (Rao & Ruderman; Cohen & Welling ICML 2014; Sohl-Dickstein et al.)** — learn generators of data transformations. *GVF's generators act on the embedding sphere and exist only as a train-time proxy generator; nothing group-structured is deployed.*
17. **Deep LDA (Dorfer et al., ICLR 2016)** — pools within-class scatter, maximizes generalized eigenvalues. *Pooling is not constraining: GVF imposes a hard rank-$K$ shared parameterization instead of optimizing a pooled-scatter ratio.*
18. **MMCR / manifold capacity (Yerxa et al. NeurIPS 2023; Chung et al. PRX 2018)** — maximize nuclear norm of augmentation-manifold centroids to raise capacity. *Capacity objectives control manifold radius and dimension magnitudes; GVF constrains manifold orientation sharing and leaves magnitudes entirely to the base field.*
19. **Kornblith et al. (NeurIPS 2021)** — higher class separation on training classes ⇒ worse transfer. *Supporting evidence for the error mode, not a competing method.*

## 4. Decisive matched-compute controls

All controls: same code, same seeds, same epoch budget, same evaluation cadence, same FLOPs unless noted.

| ID | Control | What it kills | Predicted |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | PFML verbatim, $M=15/15/2$ (in-house reproduction) | the reference | CUB 72.6 |
| **C2** | **Per-class frames**: identical generation formula, but $\Omega_j,\tau_j$ free per class (same $M$, same FLOPs, $C\times$ parameters) | **the tying mechanism itself — THE decisive control** | ≈ C1 |
| **C3** | Random frozen frame ($\Omega\!\sim\!\mathcal N$, never updated; $\tau$ learned) | "any proxy manifold / label smoothing / proxy jitter" | ≈ C1 + 0.1 |
| **C4a** | Ambient frame: $p=\mathrm{normalize}(\hat\mu_j\pm\theta_k\hat w_k)$, no tangent projection | the spherical geometry, isolated from sharing | ≈ C1 + 0.2 |
| **C4b** | Canonical parallel transport $\Gamma_{o\to\mu}$ instead of projection | the projection-vs-transport choice | ≈ GVF, higher variance |
| **C5** | **Adversarial signal frame**: $\hat w$ frozen to the top-$K$ eigenvectors of the *between*-class scatter, refreshed each epoch | §2.3's sign prediction | **below C1** |
| **C6** | $\eta=0$ (no Locus 2); and Locus 2 only | locus decomposition | $\eta{=}0$: GVF − 0.4 |
| **C7** | $\beta\equiv1$; and $\beta\to0$ | the anneal | both < GVF |
| **C8** | C1 + a frozen unused $512\times7$ tensor | the parameter-count objection (vacuous at 3.6k params, reported to close it quantitatively) | ≡ C1 |
| **C9** | $K\in\{1,3,7,15\}$ at fixed $M{=}15$ (pad with duplicated proxies) | **de-confounds proxy count $M$ from frame rank $K$** | peak at $K{=}7$ |

C2 and C5 are the two that matter. C2 holds proxy count, FLOPs and the generation formula fixed and varies *only* whether the frame is shared — if C2 ≥ GVF, the method's entire thesis is false. C5 holds everything fixed and flips the frame's *content* to the subspace the derivation says is harmful — if C5 ≈ GVF, the frame's content is irrelevant and any gain is incidental.

**Falsification thresholds (any one falsifies the method as stated):**

- **F2:** $\frac1K\sum_k\theta_k<0.02$ rad by epoch 50 in $\ge$3/5 seeds — the frame switches itself off.
- **F3:** mean $|\cos|$ between $\mathrm{span}(\hat w)$ and the top-$K$ between-class scatter eigenvectors $>0.5$ — the frame captured signal, contradicting §2.3. **This falsifies the mechanism even if R@1 improves.**
- **F4:** C3 (random frozen frame) within 0.2 of GVF on both CUB and Cars — the gain is proxy jitter, not a learned nuisance subspace.

## 6. Cost, and the traps that make "harmless" normalization operational

**Training cost.** Proxy construction $C\!\cdot\!K\!\cdot\!d=0.36$ MFLOP/step; QR of $512\times7$ negligible; Locus 2 $|B|^2(2K{+}1)d\approx126$ MFLOP/step. ResNet-50 at 224 with batch 128 is $\approx1.57$ TFLOP/step forward+backward. Arithmetic overhead **≈0.008%**; measured wall-clock overhead, dominated by kernel launches, **≈1.02×**; memory **≈1.00×** (+3.6k parameters, +1 MB of similarity scalars). Compare PA+DADA at 1.06× epoch time / 1.01× memory, and AdvRF/VAPNet which add whole auxiliary networks.

**Deployment cost: identical to the baseline.** One ResNet-50 forward, one 2048→512 linear, 512-D descriptor, same index size, same latency. $\mu,\Omega,\tau$ are deleted.

**Full experiment budget:** ≈230 A100-hours (~10 A100-days): 176 h for {GVF, C1, C2} × 4 datasets × 5 seeds; 26 h for C3–C9 × {CUB, Cars} × 3 seeds; 28 h for class-holdout hyperparameter selection.

**Three non-obvious regularizer traps (none is harmless):**

1. **Weight decay on $\tau$ *inflates* the frame.** With $\theta_k=\theta_{\max}\sigma(\tau_k)$ and $\tau_k(0)=-1.075$, decay pulls $\tau\to0$, i.e. $\theta\to\theta_{\max}/2=0.39$ rad — nearly **doubling** the amplitude rather than shrinking it. $\tau$ is excluded from weight decay.
2. **Weight decay on $\Omega$ silently raises the frame's learning rate.** QR makes $Q$ invariant to $\|\Omega\|$, so decay looks like a no-op on the frame — but $\partial Q/\partial\Omega$ scales as $1/\|\Omega\|$, so shrinking $\Omega$ monotonically amplifies the frame's effective step size over training and eventually destabilizes the QR. $\Omega$ is excluded from weight decay and its columns are renormalized to unit norm after each step.
3. **$\mu$'s radial magnitude is operational under Adam.** $\hat\mu=\mu/\|\mu\|$ hides radial drift from the loss, but Adam's step is taken in $\mu$-space, so $\|\mu\|$ drift silently rescales the *angular* step at the 100× proxy learning rate. $\mu$ is re-projected to the sphere after every step. **Whether PFML does this is undisclosed** — both variants are run and reported (§7).

**Benchmark and contamination risks:**

- **ImageNet-1K pretraining leakage.** ImageNet-1K contains ~59 bird classes and car-related classes; CUB and Cars *test* identities are semantically covered by that supervision. Permitted by the lane and shared by every reference, so *comparisons* are fair, but the absolute "zero-shot" framing is inflated by an unquantified amount. SOP's web-scraped product images may literally overlap ImageNet images; unquantified.
- **Best-epoch-on-test selection is the field's convention and is test-set model selection.** With ~40 correlated evaluation points and per-epoch test R@1 sd ≈0.4, the max inflates the reported number by roughly +0.5 to +0.9 — **the same order as the claimed +0.7 improvement.** Mitigation: identical cadence and epoch count for every arm; hyperparameters selected on a **class-disjoint holdout of training classes** (CUB: train on classes 1–80, select on 81–100, then retrain on 1–100 with a frozen epoch count); both best-epoch and fixed-epoch numbers reported, with the frontier claim made only under the convention the references used.
- **CUB test-set sampling noise.** 5,924 test images at $p{=}0.73$ gives a binomial se of **0.58 points** — larger than the 0.3 seed sd. A +0.7 gain is therefore reliable *on this test set* but is within one sampling se of "birds in general". The same caveat applies to every number in the table, including the references'.
- **SOP near-duplicate items across product IDs** cap achievable R@1 and interact specifically with PFML's decay design, which was motivated by label noise — so SOP gains may reflect noise robustness rather than the claimed mechanism. C5 is the discriminator there.
- **In-Shop's 93.0 reference has no reported uncertainty**, so no crossing can be claimed regardless of outcome.
- Exact split sources (CUB-200-2011 official; Cars196 classes 1–98/99–196; SOP and In-Shop official Ebay/DeepFashion lists) are pinned by checksum.

## 7. Unresolved source ambiguities — stated plainly

1. **PFML's undisclosed recipe:** batch size and sampler composition, LR schedule, weight decay, augmentation, per-dataset $\alpha$ and $\delta$ (only ranges $\{0,..,6\}$ and $[0.1,0.3]$ are given), proxy initialization, whether proxies are re-projected to the sphere, GAP vs GAP+GMP head, BN freezing, and whether the reported 73.4/92.7/82.9 are best-epoch or last-epoch. **Each is swept or fixed and reported; no frontier is inherited without the in-house reproduction (C1).** If C1 lands more than 1.5 points below the published number, the reproduction is declared failed and no frontier claim is made at all.
2. **Spherical Feature Transform (ECCV 2020) is my nearest work and I could not machine-read its PDF.** My distinction (nonparametric sample-to-sample rotation for feature augmentation vs. a learned low-rank shared subspace re-parameterizing the proxy set) rests on its abstract and secondary summaries. **This is the single most likely place the novelty claim breaks and the reviewer should check it first.**
3. **SoftTriple's $\lambda{=}20,\gamma{=}0.1,\delta{=}0.01,\tau{=}0.2, K{=}10$** are from the paper and official repo defaults; used only in the C-variant reduction.
4. **PA+DADA's In-Shop 93.0** has unreported seeds and uncertainty (as given in the brief), which is why no In-Shop crossing is claimed.
5. Whether Lane A permits a GAP+GMP concatenated head: I assume **GAP only** and report the GAP+GMP variant separately rather than mixing conventions.
6. I did not verify PFML's In-Shop numbers; the In-Shop comparison is against PA+DADA only.

## What I am confident in, and what I am not

Confident: the causal error mode is real and independently corroborated by AdvRF's diagnosis and Kornblith et al.'s class-separation/transfer trade-off; the parameter-capacity arithmetic (122 free intra-class parameters per training image on CUB) is checkable and damning; the frame's stationary point is *derived*, not asserted, and it cannot converge to the shortcut; the cost is genuinely negligible; and F0 is a one-run, theory-derived kill test that most proposals lack.

Not confident: whether within-class residual structure on CUB is low-rank *enough* to clear the $1/(\alpha{+}2)\approx0.2$ bar — this is the method's single load-bearing empirical precondition and it may simply fail. Also plausible: the tie is too strong and underfits genuinely class-specific multimodality (sexual dimorphism in CUB is the obvious threat), in which case C2 wins and F1 fires. And the overlap with Spherical Feature Transform may be larger than I can verify blind.
