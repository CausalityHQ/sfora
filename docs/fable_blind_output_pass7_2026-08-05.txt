## Strongest near-miss: DS-NCA — Sinkhorn-equalized batch retrieval

**Definition.** ResNet-50 → GAP → linear to ℝ⁵¹²  → ℓ2-normalize, giving zᵢ ∈ S⁵¹¹. Batches of P identities × K images (e.g. 32×4). Sᵢⱼ = zᵢ·zⱼ/τ (Sᵢᵢ = −∞), Kᵢⱼ = exp(Sᵢⱼ). Apply T unrolled Sinkhorn iterations to obtain P = diag(u) K diag(v) with unit row marginals and column marginals penalized by λ·KL(colsum‖1) (unbalanced OT; λ→∞ gives exact double stochasticity). Loss: L = −(1/N) Σᵢ log Σ_{j: yⱼ=yᵢ, j≠i} Pᵢⱼ. Gradients flow through the unrolled u, v. Hyperparameters: τ ∈ [0.05, 0.1], T ∈ {1,3,5}, λ swept from 0; AdamW, standard schedule; Sinkhorn on a 128×128 matrix is <0.1% step cost; deployment is the unchanged single 512-d descriptor with cosine k-NN. At λ=0 or T=0 it reduces *exactly* to multi-positive InfoNCE/NCA — a perfect built-in ablation: the column constraint is the entire mechanism.

**Why it was strongest.** The targeted error mode is real as a phenomenon class: row-stochastic losses constrain only each query's distribution, so nothing taxes "hub" embeddings with anomalously high average similarity, and hubs are a documented retrieval failure in high-dimensional spaces. The column constraint is a genuine mathematical change, not reweighting — the scalings solve a global fixed point, coupling all queries' gradients. It is cheapest where the frontier gap is smallest (SOP/In-Shop: huge class counts, ~5 images/class, popularity effects strongest), it costs nothing, and it has a clean falsifiable ablation.

**Exact reasons it fails.**

1. **The causal premise is unmeasured on the legal benchmarks.** No primary source quantifies hub-caused R@1 loss for *supervised* R50/512 embeddings on CUB/Cars/In-Shop/SOP. All supporting evidence is cross-regime: doubly-stochastic affinity normalization mitigating hubs in *visualization* (DOSNES); robustness of DS kernel normalization to heteroskedastic noise (Landa–Coifman–Kluger, a test-time spectral result); hubness corrections like CSLS giving +1–3 in *zero-shot/cross-lingual* retrieval with *full test-gallery access*. Requirement 2 demands a defended causal error mode; I would be inventing its magnitude.
2. **Effect-size ceiling.** The best analogues are strictly better-informed (they operate on the actual test gallery) and still yield +1–3 in other domains. A train-time-only proxy, transferring through unseen identities, cannot be forecast above that — so CUB (+5–7 needed) and Cars (+4–8) are unreachable, and the SOP/In-Shop path (+1.5–2.5 needed) rests entirely on the unmeasured premise in (1). I cannot write frozen forecasts crossing 84.2/93.9 that I could defend under audit.
3. **Algebraic self-attack (mechanism decay).** At the Sinkhorn fixed point, diag scalings are per-item logit offsets bⱼ = log vⱼ that equalize column mass — i.e., DS-NCA reduces to NCA with adaptive instance-level logit adjustment. As training makes batch similarity block-diagonal (each identity's mass absorbed by its own K−1 positives, and PK sampling balances columns in expectation), the constraint deactivates precisely when the geometry crystallizes. The surviving claim is an implicit bias of early training — unverifiable a priori.
4. **Residual novelty risk.** The mechanism has named precedents outside DML (Sinkformers' doubly-stochastic attention; SwAV/SeLa equipartition; DOSNES; long-tail logit adjustment as the reduction in (3)), and I could not complete a conclusive sweep of SSL literature for an existing batch-DS contrastive loss. A load-bearing novelty claim would be an absence-of-evidence assertion.

## Runner-up near-misses (kill reasons)

- **Class-jackknife debiased proxy risk** (leave-one-class-out correction of the training objective, targeting overfitting to the specific class set): the algebra is hollow — for fixed f, the empirical risk over distinct-class tuples is already an unbiased U-statistic of the class-population risk, so the debiasing term vanishes to first order; the zero-shot gap comes from minimization coupling, and repairing the idea to act on that coupling yields episodic meta-learning across class splits, which is occupied (MLDG-style; M3L/MetaBIN in generalizable re-ID) with a documented +1–2 history.
- **Shared within-identity transport fields** (identity-agnostic Lie-algebra vector fields modeling pose/viewpoint variation, replayed across identities): mechanism-equivalent up to parameterization to the shared-intra-class-variance/synthesis family (DVML, HDML, Proxy Synthesis, MemVir), whose documented ceiling in this lane is +1–3; the "shared across identities" identifiability assumption is exactly what that family already exploits.
- **Split-swap similarity agreement** (two proxy heads on disjoint class halves, cross-half similarity consistency): a variant of episodic meta-learning — occupied, modest gains.

Resolving this requires reading the actual results tables in the CVF HTML, not a PDF summarizer.

**Blocker 2 — the structural obstruction, which is the real reason.**

Every candidate mechanism I generated dies on one algebraic fact:

> Any supervision signal that is a **function of the seen class label** can only constrain a rank-≤(C−1) subspace of the 512-d descriptor. On CUB, C = 100, so at most 99 of 512 directions are identifiable from labels, under *any* loss — contrastive, proxy, angular, hierarchical, spectral, or hyperbolic. Re-weighting, mining, margin design, and proxy geometry all permute mass **inside** that subspace; none of them can populate the remaining ~413 directions, because those directions have literally zero gradient from a label-derived objective.

Escaping therefore requires supervision that **varies within a class**. The legal sources are exhaustively enumerable under the stated constraints: (i) instance discrimination, (ii) reconstruction/input-information preservation, (iii) augmentation parameters. And each one is *anti-correlated with the deployed objective by construction*: they re-inject exactly the within-class variation that a single-descriptor cosine retriever must ignore. So every method in this family reduces to a tug-of-war balanced by a scalar hyperparameter — which is the definition of "renaming regularization," explicitly excluded by the brief.

This also explains why the frontier paper is shaped the way it is: AdvRF attacks the identical causal error mode ("supervision from predefined categories introduces category-specific semantics… hindering generalization to unseen categories") and pays for it with a **three-network train-time pipeline** — reconstruction model + adversarial refinement + distillation into the deployed retriever. It buys within-class-varying supervision with reconstruction, at multiple-× train cost. I could not find a mechanism that buys the same thing at ~1× without collapsing into that tug-of-war.

### Strongest near-miss: nuisance-transplant consistency

The most novel-*looking* thing I derived, stated precisely so it can be killed precisely.

Batch of P classes × K samples. Embed and L2-normalize, `z = f_θ(x)/‖f_θ(x)‖`. Per class, mean direction `μ_c = Σz_i/‖Σz_i‖`. Tangential residual `u_i = (I − μ_cμ_c^ᵀ)z_i`. Transport to a common pole via the closed-form rotation `R_c` taking `μ_c → e` (differentiable, O(d)), giving `r_i = R_c u_i`. Then **graft class *a*'s nuisance onto class *b*'s identity**: `ẑ = normalize(μ_b + R_b^{-1} r_i^{(a)})`, and require `ẑ` to be classified as class *b* with the same margin as a real class-*b* sample.

The intent: if within-class residuals are genuinely identity-independent, they must be transplantable. Satisfying this certifies "identity = mean direction, nuisance = a shared fiber," which is exactly the structure under which cosine-NN transfers to identities never seen. It is cheap (~1.05× — no second network, closed-form rotations, discarded at test), and deployment is untouched.

**Why it fails — the algebra kills it.** The objective is *minimized by collapse*. If `‖r‖ → 0`, then `ẑ → μ_b` exactly, which is classified as *b* with **maximal** margin. So the loss has a global optimum at total within-class variability collapse — precisely the neural-collapse pathology it was invented to prevent. It is not merely weak against the degenerate solution; it actively rewards it. Repairing this requires bolting on a variance floor on `‖r‖`, at which point the entire mechanism is carried by the floor — i.e. it *is* the renamed regularization, and the transplant is decoration.

Two secondary kills, either sufficient on its own: the transplant operation is mechanism-equivalent to **Delta-encoder (NeurIPS 2018)**, which transfers intra-class deltas from seen to unseen classes, and to **Embedding Expansion (CVPR 2020) / HDML (CVPR 2019)**, which synthesize embedding-space points for DML — the only distinction I could offer is "used as a constraint rather than as augmentation," which the brief names as a disqualifying rename.

### The other two candidates, and why they are also occupied

Stated only to show the search was closed, not as alternatives:

- **Within-class covariance isotropy (drive Σ_W → I, not → 0).** Mechanistically sound and it *is* the right anti-collapse target — but it is Deep LDA (Dorfer et al., ICLR 2016) end-to-end, and WCCN/PLDA in speaker and face verification classically. The identifiability argument (isotropic nuisance ⇒ cosine is Bayes-optimal) is PLDA's textbook derivation, not a new one.
- **Fixed i.i.d.-random class targets instead of maximally-separated ETF prototypes** (rationale: unseen classes arrive as i.i.d. interleaved draws, not as lattice-complementary points, so ETF training creates a geometry with no room for them). This distinction is sharp and I believe under-stated — but random fixed prototypes are already an explicit variant in Hyperspherical Prototype Networks (NeurIPS 2019) and "Fix your classifier," and I have no primary evidence that target-configuration statistics causally control unseen-class R@1. Unfalsifiable at this stage, so undefendable.
