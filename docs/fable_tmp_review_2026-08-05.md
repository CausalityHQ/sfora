# Adversarial Review — Tied-Mode Proxies (TMP)

## VERDICT: LIVE

**Single strongest decisive reason:** no primary source occupies the proposal's core object — a norm-budgeted, cross-class-tied additive parameterization of multi-proxies inside a proxy-anchor loss, train-only, deployed as a vanilla 512-D cosine model — and the frozen design already contains the matched-compute control (C2: free multi-proxy at identical K and <3% cost delta) plus pre-registered falsifiers F1–F4 that make the conjecture empirically decidable at ~1.03× Proxy-Anchor cost. It is a legitimate, unoccupied, falsifiable Lane-A bet with honestly stated odds (P(crossing) ≈ 0.4–0.45 by its own account). The strongest counter-argument for DEAD — that the mechanism-forcing claim is mathematically false (below) — does not kill it, because the frozen artifact itself treats the mechanism as refutable (C7/F4) rather than relying on the false guarantee.

---

## 1. Formulas, bounds, counts, gradients

**Verified correct:** the loss is the standard Proxy-Anchor form (Kim et al., CVPR 2020) with LSE-pooled per-class similarity; the pooling bound `S_c ≤ max_k⟨z,p_ck⟩` holds (mean-normalized LSE), with `S_c ≥ max − (ln K)/β ≈ max − 0.104` at β=20, K=8, so it behaves as a soft max as claimed. Denominator range [1−ρ−ρ_ε, 1+ρ+ρ_ε] = [0.67, 1.33] ✓. Parameter counts ✓: CUB 512·(100+8+800) = 464,896 ≈ 0.46 M; Cars 98×8 = 784 pooled proxies; SOP 11,318×2 = 22,636 vs PA's 11,318 ✓. Chord ρ=0.3 ↔ ~17.25° ✓ (D6). D5 (anchor-norm inflation killed by projection) is correct and genuinely load-bearing. The gradient story for `r_k` (pooled across ~C× more data than any per-class proxy) is sound.

**Defect 1 — D1 is off by ~2×.** The bound 2ρ_ε/(1−ρ−ρ_ε) ≈ 0.09 is the ε-induced cosine shift of *one* proxy. A *between-class margin* involves both classes' residuals, so ε can manufacture up to ~0.17–0.18 cosine units of margin — comparable to the loss's own working margin 2δ = 0.2, and not "well under" the asserted (and uncited) 0.25–0.45 inter-class proxy margins. The conclusion "identity cannot hide in ε" survives only as an optimizer-pressure argument (D2's separating gradient gives no incentive to merge anchors), not as the stated proof.

## 2. Does the parameterization force a class-independent mode response? **No.**

This is the proposal's central overstatement. The objective is **exactly invariant to per-class permutations of mode assignment**: for any class c, relabeling which sample cluster uses which offset (with ε re-indexed) leaves the proxy *set* {q_c + r_k + ε_ck} unchanged; LSE pooling is symmetric in k, and the batch-mean usage balance Ω_u is unaffected when usage stays balanced. So every "aligned" optimum coexists with a combinatorial family (∏_c K!) of misaligned optima at identical loss. The parameterization shares the offset *vectors*; it cannot force their *semantics* to align across classes. Alignment can only come from encoder inductive bias — plausibly seeded by the PCA initialization (which starts at the common-principal-subspace solution) — but that is an empirical hypothesis, not the claimed forcing.

Worse, D1–D6 never address a **loss-reducing** coding shortcut: use the r_k as coarse *class-group separators* — each class parks all its samples at one mode, groups of ~C/K classes share each offset, batch-mean usage stays uniform, and inter-group margins grow by up to ~2ρ. D4's defense ("class-correlated directions cost other classes") is false for this solution: a group offset helps every class in its group and costs none. D2's pigeonhole excludes *unique* identity coding in K modes, not partial group coding. Under this optimum the claimed zero-shot mechanism is absent entirely. The saving grace is that C7/F4 would detect it (no cross-mode positive-distance signature), which is why this defect downgrades the theory section without killing the proposal.

## 3. Causal identifiability without factor annotations: partial at best

The "class-independent mode response" is identified only up to the permutation ambiguity above. C7 is model-relative — k̂ is defined via TMP's own r_k, and the frozen text does not specify what partition defines "cross-mode" for the C2 comparison model, leaving analyst degrees of freedom in the decisive causal audit. A generic within-class compactness improvement (regularization, not directional transfer) could produce a positive C7 signal. F1–F3 are crisp; F4 is the softest falsifier.

## 4. Prior art (independently searched)

No occupying instance found. Closest checks:

- **PFML (CVPR 2025)** — potential-field interaction law over free proxies representing sub-populations; no cross-class tying. The "15 proxies per class" figure could not be verified from the abstract (PDF access blocked); immaterial to the verdict, but the proposal's 0.77 M comparison rests on it.
- **DAS (ECCV 2022)** — *missing from the proposal's search*: its Memorized Transformation Shifting transfers embedding-space transformations across anchors, i.e. cross-class intra-class-variation transfer *inside DML*. It is sample-synthesis machinery with free proxies, not a proxy parameterization — so it narrows but does not occupy the claim. The proposal's novelty section covers the few-shot hallucination family but overlooked this DML-internal line.
- **GroupFace (CVPR 2020)** — K=32 latent group-aware representations shared across identities; feature-side branches *deployed at inference*, groups are sample-level clusters, not intra-class modes. Missing citation; not occupying.
- **Hierarchical proxy losses (WACV 2022; DSP 2023)** — proxies share coarse *ancestors* across class groups: shared identity-side structure, not shared intra-class mode offsets.
- **DDML (AAAI 2025)** — class-agnostic feature regularization via information bottleneck; disentangles features, does not parameterize proxies as anchor + tied offsets.
- **Sub-center ArcFace, SoftTriple, CAP** — free sub-centers (CAP additionally needs camera labels, unavailable here) — as the proposal states. **Tied factor analysis (Prince et al.), PLDA, CPC** — annotated-factor generative backends; the proposal correctly concedes these occupy the *prior*, not the object. Two uncited structural ancestors worth noting: residual/additive quantization (shared residual codebooks over coarse centroids — unsupervised, different purpose) and low-rank classifier factorizations (shared basis with free class coefficients — which is precisely TMP's C6 control endpoint, not TMP itself).

## 5. Controls: strong, with one attribution hole

The suite is a ¾-complete factorial over {tied, free} × {budgeted, unbudgeted} (TMP, C4, C2), plus random-direction (C5), magnitude-gating (C6), dose-response (C3), and audit (C7) — unusually good. The hole: the *budgeted-but-untied* cell is absent, and the frozen confirmation rule ("TMP > C2 and TMP > C5 + ladder + C7") never requires TMP > C4. So a gain driven purely by the norm budget (compactness regularization) with permuted or group-coded modes could satisfy the frozen rule and be miscredited to tying. C4 is reported but not decision-relevant as frozen. Consequently a "mechanism confirmed" outcome would be contestable even if the frontier numbers land; the frontier claim itself is unaffected.

## 6. Forecasts and lane arithmetic

Internally consistent and lane-correct: 0.912 + 0.8 + 1.1 = 0.931 (Cars), 0.925 + 0.4 + 0.7 = 0.936 (In-Shop), C2 forecasts match the decomposition; ResNet-50/512-D/224 px/single crop/cosine/151 ≤ 200 epochs; proxies discarded at deployment; no cross-lane comparisons; CUB parity and SOP no-claim are honest. Crossing thresholds are properly conservative (Cars ≥ 0.933 ≈ 2σ_ref; In-Shop ≥ 0.935 against a seedless 0.930). The stated P(Cars mean > 0.927) ≈ 0.6 is *more* conservative than its own interval implies (~0.75–0.8) — coherent only as a mixture over mechanism failure; conservative direction, acceptable. Two soft spots: the PA reproduction commitment (Cars 0.912 at R50-512) sits ~2–3 pts above widely reproduced PA results, and the whole additive tower rests on it (they do gate on it explicitly); and the +1.1 tying term has no derivation and exceeds the entire occupied multi-proxy effect (+0.8) — flagged as speculative by the proposal itself.

## 7. Hidden risks

Data/inference legality is clean: train-only proxies, PCA init from training images, hyperparameter selection on held-out *training* classes, no transduction, single-view deployment. Residual risks: C7's under-specification (item 3); recipe-vs-published-reference mismatch (acknowledged); CUB/ImageNet contamination (lane-shared); In-Shop K=8 with ~6.5 images/class leaves most (c,k) ε-cells empty — harmless but the K choice there is under-supported. One hygiene note: the "frozen" artifact contains non-scientific session residue (an operator-ping paragraph and connector-status remarks), which indicates imperfect freezing discipline but does not affect validity.

---

**Bottom line:** LIVE. Run it as frozen. The novelty object stands after an independent sweep, the falsifiers are real, and the cost is negligible. But two claims inside the artifact should be discounted in advance: the "proof-level" forcing of a class-independent mode response is false (exact permutation invariance; unaddressed group-coding shortcut), and a mechanism confirmation under the frozen decision rule would be attributable to the norm budget rather than to tying. If the protocol's bar for LIVE required the mechanism *argument* to be sound as written — not merely decidable by the frozen experiments — the permutation-invariance defect would be the DEAD-maker; on my reading of the bar, it is the proposal's chief liability, not its death.
