# In-Shop So400m 384 vs 256 px fine-tuned TRAIN pilot — pre-registration (2026-10-24)

Trigger: the zero-training screen (447bddc4) gave ADVANCE. Pretrained So400m at 384 beat 256 by +5.67 pp packed full-TRAIN R@1.
All earlier In-Shop So400m training (native256 campaign, official 96.244) ran at 256.

- Trainer: scripts/train_inshop_so400m_pilot.py. This is the SOP 384/512-D frontier recipe: float-rank member bank, ArcFace 0.3/64 + 8 × SmoothAP, bf16, coverage-first, 13 of 27 blocks frozen, 1000 updates, batch 64. 512-D head, packed int8 (514 B/row).
- Data: In-Shop TRAIN only, the screen partition. Fit 12,761 rows (singleton products are gallery-only). Held 13,121 queries vs all 25,882 TRAIN rows. Query/gallery splits never read.
- Arms: crop 256 (control) and crop 384 (candidate), seed 179024. Each is head-initialized from its own pretrained screen features.
- Decision (scripts/screen_inshop_pilot_pair_v1.py, product bootstrap 5000): PASS iff the held R@1 paired lower95 is > 0 AND the mAP@R upper95 is >= 0.
  - PASS → train ONE full-TRAIN (all 3,985 products with ≥ 2 images) So400m@384/512-D model on the same recipe, then ONE official In-Shop read. That model is retained as a candidate iff its official R@1 > 96.307 (current incumbent); it meets the target iff its R@1 > 96.7.
  - KILL → close the In-Shop resolution lane.
- Known risk: on In-Shop, TRAIN-held gains have transferred poorly to official (+0.67 held → +0.01 official for the substrate swap). The official read is the arbiter, and it is read once.

## PASS-branch command, fixed before any pilot number was read
`train_inshop_so400m_pilot.py --crop 384 --seed 179024 --updates 1000 --full-train` trains on all TRAIN products with ≥ 2 images (single-image products have no positive). It uses the identical recipe, with PCA/bank init from the r384 pretrained features. It then makes exactly ONE official read: 14,218 queries vs 12,612 gallery, packed int8 512-D, exact torch R@1 / mAP@R (`official_read`).

## Efficiency point: In-Shop 280 px (pre-registered 2026-10-24, before any 280 number)
- **Why:** 384 serves at 68 img/s. SOP at 280 px gives 112 img/s (fp16) for −0.49 official R@1.
- **Pilot:** the same pilot command with `--crop 280`, seed 179024, and the same partition.
  - Head/bank initialization uses the 384 pretrained features. This is a documented deviation, the same one the SOP 280 wrapper uses.
- **Comparison:** against the existing 384 pilot receipt, using `screen_inshop_pilot_pair_v1.py` (candidate = 280, control = 384).
- **Rule to ADVANCE:** paired held R@1 lower95 > −0.30 pp (non-inferiority vs 384).
  - On ADVANCE: train one full-TRAIN 280 model and make ONE official read.
  - It is retained as the efficient Pareto point iff official R@1 > 96.7. Its serving is then measured with `check_inshop_serving_v1.py`, adapted to 280.
  - Otherwise: KILL, and the 384 model stays the only In-Shop point.

## Efficiency lever 2: Large/16 @384 (pre-registered 2026-10-25, before any Large@384 number)
- **Evidence:** at matched 256 px, fine-tuned So400m ≈ Large on In-Shop official (96.244 vs 96.230). The In-Shop gain came from 384 px.
  - Large@384 is 576 tokens × 24 × 1024, about 0.55× the So400m@384 encoder FLOPs.
- **Step 1, zero-training screen:** `screen_inshop_large_resolution_v1.py`, ADVANCE iff Large r384 − r256 ≥ +1.0 pp.
- **Step 2, pilot:** `train_inshop_so400m_pilot.py --substrate large --crop 384`, seed 179024, same partition and recipe.
  - Large arm table: 12 of 24 blocks frozen. Head/bank initialization from the screen's Large r384 features, sha pinned in `LARGE_FEATURES_SHA256` before the run.
  - Compared with the existing So400m@384 pilot receipt via `screen_inshop_pilot_pair_v1.py` (candidate = Large).
  - ADVANCE iff paired held R@1 lower95 > −0.30 pp, the same margin as the 280 px test.
- **Step 3, full TRAIN:** Large@384 `--full-train`, ONE official read. Retained as the efficient Pareto point iff all hold:
  - official R@1 > 96.7;
  - paired official R@1 vs the protocol-matched Large/256/128 control (native256-full-v1) has lower95 > 0;
  - the FP8 serving pipeline is > 1.2× the So400m FP8 end-to-end img/s (101.3).
  - Otherwise So400m@384 stays the only In-Shop point.

## Efficiency lever 3: distil So400m@384 into Large/16@384 (pre-registered 2026-10-25, before any number)
- **Evidence:** fine-tuned Large@384 lost −0.67 held R@1 vs So400m@384. Pretrained Large@384 encodes 1.83× faster.
- **Teacher:** the So400m@384 pilot (`sfora-inshop-so400m-c384-179024-1000-v1`, trained on fit products only). It provides `train_embeddings.npy`, clean views, all TRAIN rows; only fit rows are used.
- **Student:** `--substrate large --crop 384 --teacher-embeddings <teacher> --distill-weight 1.0`, seed 179024, same partition and recipe.
- **Pilot rule:** ADVANCE iff the paired held R@1 vs the So400m@384 pilot has lower95 > −0.30 pp, the same margin as before.
  - ADVANCE leads to a full-TRAIN student distilled from the shipped full-TRAIN teacher.
  - That student is then scored with ONE official read under the Step-3 rules of efficiency lever 2: R@1 > 96.7, lower95 > 0 vs the matched Large/256 control, and FP8 serving > 1.2× of 101.3 img/s.
- Also reported (not gating): the student vs the undistilled Large@384 pilot (97.94).
