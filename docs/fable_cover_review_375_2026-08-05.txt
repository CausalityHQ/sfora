**Verdict: DEAD.**

The proposal fails on its own mathematics before any experiment is run: the load-bearing "reuse floor" term is unsatisfiable as written and degenerates into a duplicate uniformity penalty, the "cannot cheat" claim is false because template solutions remain realizable (and are actively favored by the loss geometry and the tuning protocol), and the frontier-crossing forecasts rest on an accounting argument with no measured premises while the closest measured anchors point the other way. Details follow.

## 1. Objective algebra: the reuse floor is broken (decisive)

The definition is u_j = Σ_c A_{jc}/‖A_c‖₁ with floor m ≈ ⌈0.6·Cs/k⌉. Since A ≥ 0, this usage is class-mass-normalized, so Σ_j u_j = C identically. On CUB (C=100, s=24, k=512): m = 3, so satisfying the floor requires Σ_j u_j ≥ k·m = 1536, but the total is fixed at 100. On In-Shop (C=3997, s=8): m = 38 versus a fixed total of 3997 against a requirement of 19,456. The floor is unsatisfiable in every configuration the proposal specifies — every atom is below it at all times. Minimizing Σ_j max(0, m−u_j)² subject to Σ_j u_j = C gives (by symmetric convexity) uniform u_j = C/k: the term reduces to a second uniformity pressure duplicating λ_bal's KL term, plus a large constant. The advertised semantics — "an atom serving fewer than m classes is taxed, so class-private template atoms are uneconomical" — is not implemented by the written loss. The floor value 0.6·Cs/k is calibrated for a *class-count* usage (mean count Cs/k ≈ 4.7 on CUB), while the formula computes *mass* usage (mean C/k ≈ 0.195): a unit error of exactly factor s. Both candidate fixes are substantive: a count-based u_j is non-differentiable and, worse, trivially gameable, because top-s masking of softplus keeps entries strictly positive but arbitrarily small — an atom can "serve" m classes with ε mass and remain a private template. Per the rules, fixing this is a different proposal.

## 2. Degenerate solutions: the no-cheat crux is false

Even granting an intended reuse constraint, the template endpoint the proposal claims to excise remains inside the hypothesis class, and three forces select for it:

- **Combinatorial capacity.** On CUB, k=512 leaves ~5 atoms per class for C=100. Codes with effective mass on ~5 private atoms (the other top-s entries near zero) give near-orthogonal proxies: two random 24-sparse codes share s²/k ≈ 1.1 atoms in expectation, Bhattacharyya overlap ≈ 0.05. The simplex-ETF template solution reappears *inside the code space*, with the backbone learning per-patch class-identity detectors — realizable, since a stride-1 conv5 receptive field at 224² covers most of the image.
- **Loss geometry.** z and π live on the nonnegative orthant, so cosine ∈ [0,1] and Proxy-Anchor's negative margin (s ≤ −δ = −0.1) is unreachable; the negative term is minimized only at exact orthogonality, i.e., disjoint supports. At α=32 this exerts persistent, strong pressure toward class-private codes — directly against the intended sharing. The orthant interaction is never addressed.
- **Tuning protocol.** λ_bal, λ_use, λ_coh are tuned on held-out *seen* classes, whose retrieval is maximized precisely by discriminative/private solutions. The selection criterion is blind to the mechanism's only claimed benefit, so hyperparameter search itself will dial the sharing pressure down.

Also note the balance/usage terms act only on class codes; nothing constrains image-side atom usage or the gate, so image histograms can concentrate freely. The claim "a class can only be separated by differing usage of atoms that ≥m other classes also use" is false for the frozen object; if SGD's simplicity bias prefers templates (the proposal's own §2 premise), it finds the in-code-space template here, and unseen classes again land on weak mixtures of seen templates — now in histogram space. The method re-parameterizes the diagnosed disease rather than removing it.

## 3. Forecasts do not follow from measured premises

There is no pilot data; the intervals and P(cross) are priors. The §7 "excess-risk accounting" is numerology: capping the head code at s·log₂k ≈ 216 bits per class bounds nothing, because the 25M-parameter backbone upstream of the assignment remains free to implement class-specific detectors (§2); no uniform-convergence result of the invoked form exists for unseen-class DML, and none is cited. The measured anchors cut against the forecast: Proxy-Anchor with ResNet-50/512 — CoVeR's own base loss — scores 69.7 R@1 on CUB ([Kim et al., CVPR 2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.13911), [project page](https://cvlab.postech.ac.kr/research/ProxyAnchor/)), so the head swap alone must contribute ~+10 to reach 79.5, using a strictly less expressive head. The expressivity-parity claim ("GAP is also a bag statistic") is wrong: under GAP+linear each patch contributes a full continuous projected feature (W·GAP = mean of Wφ_p); under CoVeR each patch contributes a sparse simplex vector times one scalar gate. Fifteen years of aggregation evidence — BoW histograms losing to residual aggregation (VLAD/Fisher) at matched dimension, the sqrt-histogram cosine being exactly the classical Hellinger/RootSIFT geometry ([Ionescu & Popescu](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-642-41181-6_9), [All about VLAD](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/259624612_All_about_VLAD)) — plus CRT's within-DML residual finding all point the opposite way, and E²BoWs' need for class-tied words is further weak negative evidence for count descriptors at frontier level. Finally, the forecast table contradicts the proposal's own causal story: §2 argues Cars has no compositional headroom and In-Shop already shares "by accident," yet §6 assigns P(cross)=0.75 and 0.80 there, invoking an ad-hoc variance-reduction face. On In-Shop the pigeonhole genuinely binds (3997 classes cannot own 512 atoms; ≥8 classes must share each dominant atom), which spends seen-class fit exactly where instance-level detail (prints, patterns) decides R@1. The DINO/ViT "natural experiment" cannot carry the compositional attribution either — it changes pretraining data, objective, and architecture simultaneously, with Cars near ceiling.

## 4. Controls are weaker than claimed

Control #3's non-monotone k signature is claimed unique; it is the generic codebook-size/dimension bias–variance curve known since classical BoW and general in VQ. Worse, k *is* the deployed dimension (z ∈ R^k; k=512 was chosen to sit in the 512-d tier), so the sweep confounds vocabulary size with descriptor capacity and exits the matched-dimension tier — it cannot isolate the mechanism. Control #1's attribution ("isolates sparsity+reuse") is compromised by §1: the "reuse" term actually implements uniformity, so full-versus-dense differences cannot be credited to reuse. Controls #2 and #4 and the F1–F5 battery are genuinely good preregistration practice — but on the analysis above F5 (CUB < 77.1) is the expected outcome, and falsifiability does not repair the forward claim.

## 5. Budget and protocol

Deployment compliance is fine (single 512-d cosine vector, single crop; no external data/teacher/test adaptation). The cost claim is not: stride-1 conv5 quadruples conv5 FLOPs (~0.8 → ~3.2 GFLOPs on a ~4.1 GFLOP model), roughly +55–60% per step, not the claimed +25–30%; with entmax and "100–200 epochs" against typical DML schedules, the 1.5× budget is at risk and 1.3–1.35× is not credible as frozen. Secondary, but the accounting is wrong in the proposal's favor.

## 6. Prior art

The novelty claim narrowly survives my sweep: I found no work implementing the joint mechanism (assignment-histogram descriptor + proxies as sparse nonnegative codes over the same vocabulary). The proposal's perimeter missed some neighbors worth noting — [SALAD, CVPR 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15937) deploys assignment-based aggregation with a learned dustbin (≈ the gate) as a single global descriptor; metric learning inside BoW codebook generation exists for re-ID ([here](https://deepai.org/publication/metric-learning-in-codebook-generation-of-bag-of-words-for-person-re-identification)); classes-as-codes-over-shared-components is ECOC-old — but none kills the joint claim. Novelty is not the failure mode; the algebra, the reachable optima, and the evidence chain are.

**Disposition.** DEAD as frozen. A version with a correctly specified count-plus-minimum-mass reuse constraint, an orthant-aware margin loss, image-side usage regularization, and a validation signal that can see the mechanism would be a different proposal and must be evaluated as such, per the review rules. The verified frontier context stands as given: [AdvRF](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21742), [VAPNet](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/cc19e4ffde5540ac3fcda240e6d975cb-Paper-Conference.pdf).
