## Proposed measurement and collision

Fable's fallback was a leave-classes-out headroom decomposition. It proposed
training on half the official training identities, evaluating ordinary cosine
retrieval on the other half, then fitting (a) an oracle global linear metric
with the held-out labels and (b) a nonlinear probe on part of the held-out
identity data.

This is exact candidate 371, already rejected before execution:

1. A metric fitted and scored on the same held-out identity population measures
   closed-set fitting of that estimator, not the ability of a training-side
   mechanism to transfer to unseen identities.
2. A nonlinear probe trained on half the samples from every held-out identity
   adds direct identity supervision. Its advantage mixes closed-set adaptation,
   sample splitting, model capacity, and optimization; it is not an upper bound
   on open-set method headroom.
3. Nesting an additional identity split can estimate transfer of one specified
   metric or probe, but cannot identify an intrinsic demanded geometry or prove
   that a novel training action exists.
4. Training two base models plus fitting and validating two oracle tiers is not
   the simple one-training-equivalent diagnostic claimed in the answer.

The positive action implied by the probe branch also returns to candidate 365's
occupied blind/shared-private subspace allocation or to established direct-sum
and auxiliary representation methods.
