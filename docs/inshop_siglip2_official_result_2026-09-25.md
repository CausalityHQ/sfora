The [frozen TRAIN-only three-seed decision](inshop_siglip2_paired_training_gate_2026-09-25.md) selected the bank arm before the official query/gallery read. The selected checkpoints were evaluated serially on the official DeepFashion In-Shop **14,218 queries / 12,612 gallery images**. The gallery representation is a 128-coordinate signed int8 code plus an fp16 inverse norm (**130 bytes/image**). The native scorer matched the packed-score oracle's top 10 for every query, with maximum score delta zero. This readout is exploratory because this project has had prior official In-Shop reads; it is not an independent clean confirmation or an official bank-versus-float comparison.

Every official receipt binds the TRAIN decision, training receipt/checkpoint, evaluator source, model, library and CuTile runtime. The evaluator verified its actually imported helper paths and Torch/Transformers versions before reading official images. Peak evaluator CUDA allocation was **1,886,321,152 bytes** in each run. The aggregate rule was frozen as mean packed Recall@1 with all seeds reported, never best seed.

This mean is **1.2177 percentage points below** the dated [UNICOM L14/336 published 96.7% In-Shop Recall@1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.05884) reference. The earlier local compact ViT-L/14 reproduction scored 95.4283%; this candidate is only **0.0540 percentage points above** that local result. The published UNICOM model trained on all 25,882 TRAIN images and uses a different full-width encoder/scorer, so this comparison does not isolate the method. The packed-versus-float difference within this candidate is negligible. The TRAIN holdout bank gain did not predict a quality level above the published reference.

The likely mismatch is that the TRAIN holdout used the full TRAIN gallery, including 23,342 fit-class distractors seen during training, whereas official query/gallery identities are unseen. The predeclared one-seed [TRAIN-only diagnostic](evidence/compact_metric/sop-siglip2-substrate-v1/inshop-held-only-paired-179023-v2.json) re-exported the same 2,540 held images and scored them against only those 399 held identities, self excluded. Bank scored **99.8425%** Recall@1 and float **99.6063%**, a **0.2362 percentage-point** bank gain, down from **0.6299 points** with the full TRAIN gallery. Adding fit-class distractors therefore accounts for **0.3937 points** of the observed paired gain on this one seed. This verifies a gallery-composition effect in the validation result; it does not by itself explain the official 95.4823% level. The held-only gallery is smaller and easier than official, so its absolute score is not a quality forecast. The diagnostic receipt SHA-256 is `bf710bcfc2355c3f8da0658a56863c6bbcb63d0c668a6be263e8e9cfca37fa95`; its [terminal journal](evidence/compact_metric/sop-siglip2-substrate-v1/inshop-held-only-paired-179023-v2.journal.log) has SHA-256 `8a590cd273f860cc05e7f1ee685b7a793e105666a8da240d9f74bfdd06ac02e6`. An initial [failed attempt](evidence/compact_metric/sop-siglip2-substrate-v1/inshop-held-only-paired-179023-v1-failure.journal.log) stopped before scoring because the exporter received mismatched path and label lengths; the corrected source was pushed before this run. The diagnostic cannot revise the completed bank selection.

The next production decision is to revise validation so all gallery identities are unseen at the official gallery scale, and to test a representation/training change against that gate before another official read. Official labels and scores will not choose its hyperparameters.

A separate source-ceiling [TRAIN-only readout](evidence/compact_metric/sop-siglip2-substrate-v1/inshop-source-ceiling-train-v1.json) rules out a simple claim that fine-tuning destroyed the pretrained embedding: on the same 2,540 held TRAIN queries, the untrained 1024-D SigLIP2 source scored **81.0236%** Recall@1 against full TRAIN and **93.7402%** against held-only gallery; fit-only PCA-128 float scored **77.2047% / 92.0866%**, and its packed version **76.8898% / 91.9685%**. The trained bank head reached **97.8346% / 99.8425%** on those respective one-seed galleries. These are different gallery protocols from official and do not isolate whether the *final trained* full-width source would beat the compact head. Source receipt SHA-256 `7b231b923c40aaadbb81d6d4d86bce4e2108da360a63f53e069cfc7c7cf7765a`; [terminal journal](evidence/compact_metric/sop-siglip2-substrate-v1/inshop-source-ceiling-train-v1.journal.log) SHA-256 `4b9ea4791220560a0e690aa10beebcb40fc3edda61fbc9c853dc1965dd3f4736`.

Before paying for another training arm, the next predeclared kill test will export the completed seed-179023 bank checkpoint on non-singleton fit queries against the full fit gallery, self excluded, and compare its 24 encoder blocks with the pinned pretrained weights. If fit Recall@1 is at least **99.5%**, longer training is dropped as likely memorization. If mean relative L2 drift in blocks 0–11 is at most **25%** of blocks 12–23, the lower-block-freeze arm is dropped because those blocks already barely moved. These are screening rules, not quality claims. A surviving arm will be tested against a new TRAIN-only product-disjoint gallery near the official 12,612-image scale; the prior official read cannot select it.
