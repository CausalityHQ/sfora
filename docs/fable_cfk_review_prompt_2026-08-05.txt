## 2. Causal zero-shot error mode, and a proof-level attack on the degeneracies

### 2.1 The error mode

Let a latent generative factor $s_j$ (throat colour, plumage morph, bill shape) vary *within* some seen classes. Under any isotropic proxy/softmax score, the loss for a positive is monotone decreasing in $\sum_j(z_j-\hat p_{c,j})^2$ with **equal weight on every $j$**. There is therefore no configuration in which a class is rewarded for *representing* its own internal variation; the only loss-reducing move is to make $z_j$ constant within the class, i.e. to make the backbone insensitive to $s_j$. Suppression is not a failure to learn — it is the optimum.

The zero-shot cost is direct: unseen bird species are separated chiefly by exactly the plumage/colour/pattern factors that are polymorphic *within* many seen species (sexual dimorphism, juvenile/adult morphs). Two hundred epochs of uniform compactness pressure is a sustained, systematic instruction to delete them. CFK removes the pressure specifically on the axes where the deletion is being paid for, and nowhere else.

**Scope limit, stated up front.** CFK can only rescue factors that are *present as within-class variation in the training images*. A factor absent from all seen classes (all 100 seen species white-throated) is unrecoverable by any train-time reweighting; only the ImageNet prior can carry it. I do not claim otherwise. This is why the mechanism predicts an ordered, dataset-dependent footprint (§5) rather than a uniform lift.

### 2.2 Degeneracy 1 — the geometric-mean shortcut (the real one)

**Claim.** With $\tau=\infty$, CFK collapses to a geometric mean and a *single* matching axis suffices.

*Proof.* Fix $z,\hat p_c$, write $r_j=(z_j-\hat p_{c,j})^2$. Minimize $\sum_j e^{u_j}r_j$ s.t. $\sum_j u_j=0$. Lagrangian stationarity gives $e^{u_j}r_j=\text{const}$, i.e. $e^{u_j}\propto r_j^{-1}$, with optimal value $d\big(\prod_j r_j\big)^{1/d}$. So unconstrained CFK replaces $d\cdot\mathrm{AM}(r)$ by $d\cdot\mathrm{GM}(r)$. Since $\mathrm{GM}(r)\to0$ whenever any single $r_j\to0$, a positive sample could achieve $\sigma_c\to1$ by matching the proxy exactly on one coordinate while being arbitrary on the other 511. $\square$

**The attack.** The clip $\|u_c\|_\infty\le2\tau$ makes the bound two-sided and *quantitative*:

$$
e^{-2\tau}\|z-\hat p_c\|^2\;\le\;D_c(z)\;\le\;e^{2\tau}\|z-\hat p_c\|^2
\;\Longrightarrow\;
1-\sigma_c(z)\;\ge\;e^{-2\tau}\big(1-\langle z,\hat p_c\rangle\big).
$$

With $\tau_{\max}=0.55$, $e^{-2\tau}=1/3$: **the effective margin scale can never fall below $\alpha_{\text{eff}}=\alpha/3=10.7$.** A positive still requires genuine global alignment; the shortcut is bounded, not merely discouraged. (At $\tau=1.1$, $\alpha_{\text{eff}}=3.6$ — which is why the $\tau$-sweep must show an inverted U, and a monotone-increasing sweep falsifies this whole reading; see F7.)

Second, the gauge makes the shortcut self-punishing: any axis discounted by $e^{-2\tau}$ forces compensating amplification elsewhere, and the *negative* term $e^{+\alpha\sigma_c(z^-)}$ then penalizes impostors on precisely those amplified axes. A class cannot buy positive slack without simultaneously sharpening its own impostor rejection.

### 2.3 Degeneracy 2 — precision collapse

Without the gauge, $\omega_c\to0$ drives $\sigma_c\to1$ for every sample and the loss to a constant: total representational collapse, zero backbone gradient. The hard constraint $\prod_j\omega_{c,j}=1$ makes this **exactly unreachable**, not penalized. This is the single most important structural difference from any soft log-det / entropy penalty.

### 2.4 Degeneracy 3 — class memorization

$\tilde u$ adds $Cd$ parameters (SOP: 5.8 M). It could memorize seen-class identity without improving features. Three guards: (i) $\eta_c$ shrinks hardest exactly where $n_c$ is smallest and memorization is easiest; (ii) $\lambda_u$; (iii) structurally, $\tilde u$ is discarded at test, so memorization cannot *help* zero-shot — it can only waste capacity, which shows up as CFK $\le$ PA and is caught by F1. The matched-parameter control C4 (§4) settles whether the gain is anisotropy or just $Cd$ more class parameters.

### 2.5 Symmetry, stated precisely and not overclaimed

$\mathcal L_{\mathrm{PA}}$ is **exactly invariant** under $R\in O(d)$ acting as $W\mapsto RW,\ w_0\mapsto Rw_0,\ p_c\mapsto Rp_c$ (cosines are preserved; $\|RW\|_F=\|W\|_F$ so weight decay is too). $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CFK}}$ is invariant only under signed permutations $\{\pm1\}^d\rtimes S_d$. CFK therefore removes $d(d-1)/2=130{,}816$ flat directions from the minimizer set.

**I do not use this as the load-bearing argument**, because cosine retrieval is itself rotation-invariant, so basis alignment *per se* cannot change R@1. Two honest caveats: (a) Adam's coordinate-wise preconditioner already breaks $O(d)$ weakly, but in a data- and class-independent way that cannot align coordinates with class-conditional factor structure; (b) if the released PA code places a `BatchNorm1d` on the embedding, the exact-symmetry statement is false and must be restated as approximate. The load-bearing argument is §2.1 + the gradient attenuation of §1.4, which changes *which function* is learned, not merely its coordinates.

### 3.3 Outside DML — the actual import, disclosed as prior art

* **Flury, "Common Principal Components in $k$ groups", JASA/Biometrika 1984–86.** The model CFK implicitly fits: $k$ covariance matrices sharing one eigenbasis with group-specific eigenvalues. Flury *fits* the basis to fixed data by ML; CFK *learns the representation* so the model holds, discriminatively.
* **Gales, "Semi-tied covariance matrices for HMMs", IEEE TSAP 1999; Kumar & Andreou, heteroscedastic LDA / MLLT.** Speech recognition's shared linear transform making every Gaussian diagonal. CFK replaces the linear transform with the whole ResNet-50, replaces EM with an anchor loss, and adds the sphere, the bound, and the shrinkage.
* **Nonlinear ICA with auxiliary variables** (Hyvärinen–Morioka 2016/17; Hyvärinen–Sasaki–Turner AISTATS 2019; Khemakhem et al. iVAE 2020). These give the theoretical reason to expect transfer: with an auxiliary variable modulating conditionally-factorial sources, $k=1$ (location-only) sufficient statistics are *not* identifying; $k=2$ (location **and** scale) is. Every proxy loss in DML is the $k=1$ case with the class label as auxiliary variable. CFK is the $k=2$ case. I present this as *motivation, not proof* — the identifiability theorems' diversity conditions ($dk+1$ sufficiently varied auxiliary values, exact factorial conditionals) are not verifiable on CUB, and I do not claim they hold.
* **Kent (1982) / Fisher–Bingham directional statistics** — the exact family $\exp(a_c^\top z+z^\top B_c z)$ that $\sigma_c$ instantiates with $B_c$ diagonal. (Prior deep use is concentrated in $SO(3)$/pose estimation, e.g. Deep Bingham Networks, not retrieval — but see §3.1.)

## 6. Cost, and benchmark / contamination risk

**Training.** Added parameters $Cd$: CUB 51 k, Cars 50 k, In-Shop 2.1 M, SOP 5.8 M (a sparse table, batch-sparse access). For contrast, AdvRF's train-time ResNet-34/U-Net reconstruction + distillation is a materially larger training system, and DiVA-style multi-branch methods add branches; CFK adds two GEMMs and one table.

**Deployment.** Byte-identical to the baseline: one ResNet-50, one 224-px view, one 512-D unit vector, cosine NN. Zero inference overhead, zero index change.

**Risks.**

1. **Seed noise.** All four benchmarks resolve <0.005 poorly. Everything above is 5 seeds with SD; I will not report single-seed numbers, and the In-Shop reference's missing variance is a stated limit on what can be concluded there.
2. **ImageNet-1K overlap.** CUB and Cars overlap semantically with ImageNet-1K. This is lane-standard and shared by every reference, and CFK adds no new external data. **But CFK's mechanism specifically preserves factors already latent in the initialization**, so it may benefit from that overlap *more* than the baselines do — a real confound for the mechanism claim, not for lane legality. Control: rerun CUB from random init (both C0 and CFK, extended schedule) and confirm the delta persists directionally. If it vanishes, the honest description becomes "CFK retards catastrophic forgetting of pretrained factors", which is a different and weaker claim.
3. **Hyperparameter contamination.** $\tau,\eta,\lambda_u$ will be selected on a **class-disjoint validation split carved from the training classes** (last 20 % of training identities held out as pseudo-unseen), then the model retrained on the full training split. Never on the test split. This is a common silent violation in DML and I am pre-committing against it.
4. **No transduction anywhere.** $p_c,\tilde u_c$ are discarded; no test-gallery statistics, no reranking, no query expansion, no multi-crop.
5. **Novelty risk dominates empirical risk.** See §3.1.
