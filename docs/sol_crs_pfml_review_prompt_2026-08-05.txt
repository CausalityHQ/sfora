## Proposed method: Cross-Fitted Reliability-Spectrum PFML

I choose **Lane A only**: ImageNet-1K initialized ResNet-50, 512-D normalized descriptor, 224-pixel input, 200 epochs, single-view cosine retrieval.

The method, abbreviated **CRS-PFML**, augments PFML with a cross-fitted spectral objective that rewards descriptor directions reproducible across two *different photographs of the same training identity*. Its purpose is to suppress instance-specific background, pose, crop, and augmentation shortcuts without collapsing all within-class structure.

### 1. Executable definition

Let \(f_\theta:\mathcal X\rightarrow\mathbb R^{512}\) be ResNet-50 followed by a learned \(2048\times512\) linear layer. The deployed descriptor is

\[
z_\theta(x)=\frac{f_\theta(x)}
{\max(\|f_\theta(x)\|_2,10^{-12})}.
\]

The other learned objects are PFML’s unconstrained proxy parameters \(q_{y,m}\in\mathbb R^{512}\), used as normalized proxies

\[
p_{y,m}=\frac{q_{y,m}}{\max(\|q_{y,m}\|_2,10^{-12})}.
\]

Use \(M=15\) proxies per identity on CUB/Cars and \(M=2\) on SOP, matching audited PFML.

#### Sampling

Each iteration samples \(P=32\) training identities. For every identity \(y\), sample two distinct official training photographs \(x_y^{(1)}\ne x_y^{(2)}\), uniformly without replacement. Apply independent ordinary stochastic augmentations \(T_y^{(1)},T_y^{(2)}\):

- random resized crop to \(224^2\), scale \([0.16,1]\), aspect ratio \([3/4,4/3]\);
- horizontal flip with probability \(0.5\);
- color jitter \((0.4,0.4,0.4,0.1)\) with probability \(0.8\);
- grayscale with probability \(0.2\).

Thus

\[
z_y^{(a)}=z_\theta(T_y^{(a)}x_y^{(a)}),\qquad a\in\{1,2\}.
\]

There is only one forward pass per sampled photograph; CRS uses the same 64 images that a class-balanced PFML batch would process.

#### PFML term

For distance \(d(r,u)=\|r-u\|_2\), fix \(\delta=0.2,\alpha=2\) and define

\[
\psi_{\rm att}(r,u)=
\begin{cases}
-\delta^{-\alpha},&d(r,u)<\delta,\\
-d(r,u)^{-\alpha},&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
\]

\[
\psi_{\rm rep}(r,u)=
\begin{cases}
d(r,u)^{-\alpha},&d(r,u)<\delta,\\
\delta^{-\alpha},&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

For training class \(j\),

\[
\Psi_j(r)=
\sum_{i:y_i=j}\psi_{\rm att}(r,z_i)
+\sum_{m=1}^{M}\psi_{\rm att}(r,p_{j,m})
+\sum_{i:y_i\ne j}\psi_{\rm rep}(r,z_i)
+\sum_{\gamma\ne j}\sum_{m=1}^{M}\psi_{\rm rep}(r,p_{\gamma,m}).
\]

The PFML energy is

\[
U=
\sum_{i\in\mathcal B}\Psi_{y_i}(z_i)
+\sum_j\sum_{m=1}^{M}\Psi_j(p_{j,m}).
\]

This is the published potential-field construction: its interaction strength decays with distance and all embeddings and proxies receive gradients through \(U\). [PFML gives the original equations and training setup here.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18560)

#### Cross-fitted reliability spectrum

At each iteration independently draw a Gaussian matrix \(G\in\mathbb R^{512\times16}\), compute its thin QR factorization, and retain the Haar-distributed orthonormal matrix \(R\in\mathbb R^{512\times16}\). \(R\) is not learned and receives no gradient.

Project the two independently sampled photographs:

\[
v_y^{(a)}=R^\top z_y^{(a)}\in\mathbb R^{16},
\qquad
\tilde v_y^{(a)}=v_y^{(a)}-\frac1P\sum_{j=1}^{P}v_j^{(a)}.
\]

Define

\[
S_{aa}=\frac1{P-1}\sum_y
\tilde v_y^{(a)}\tilde v_y^{(a)\top}
+\varepsilon I,
\quad \varepsilon=10^{-3},
\]

\[
S_{12}=\frac1{2(P-1)}
\sum_y\left[
\tilde v_y^{(1)}\tilde v_y^{(2)\top}
+\tilde v_y^{(2)}\tilde v_y^{(1)\top}
\right].
\]

Let \(S=(S_{11}+S_{22})/2\) and

\[
C=S^{-1/2}S_{12}S^{-1/2},
\]

where the symmetric inverse square root is computed by `eigh`, clamping eigenvalues below \(10^{-3}\). All gradients pass through centering, covariances, eigendecomposition, \(z\), and \(\theta\).

The reliability loss is

\[
L_{\rm CRS}
=
-\frac1{16}\log\det(CC^\top+10^{-3}I).
\]

Unlike a trace objective, this maximizes the *product* of the 16 canonical repeatabilities. Resampling \(R\) exposes all 512 descriptor directions over training and prevents the optimizer from concentrating repeatable information in a small fixed subspace.

For numerical scaling, maintain detached exponential moving averages

\[
m_U\leftarrow .99m_U+.01|U|,\qquad
m_C\leftarrow .99m_C+.01L_{\rm CRS},
\]

initialized from the first batch. Optimize the single objective

\[
L_t=\frac{U}{m_U+10^{-12}}
+\lambda_t\frac{L_{\rm CRS}}{m_C+10^{-12}},
\]

with

\[
\lambda_t=
\begin{cases}
0,&t<10,\\
0.1\,\frac{t-10}{20},&10\le t<30,\\
0.1,&t\ge30,
\end{cases}
\]

where \(t\) is epoch number. No gradient flows through \(m_U,m_C,\lambda_t\).

Train for 200 epochs with Adam: backbone/projection learning rate \(5\cdot10^{-4}\), proxy learning rate \(5\cdot10^{-2}\), cosine decay to zero, weight decay \(10^{-4}\) on \(\theta\) and zero on proxies. Hyperparameters remain fixed across datasets.

At test time, discard proxies, CRS calculations, random projections, and augmentations. Resize to \(256^2\), take one \(224^2\) center crop, compute one 512-D \(z_\theta(x)\), and rank by cosine similarity. This exactly satisfies Lane A deployment.

### 2. Causal error mode and degeneracies

Suppose a projected descriptor admits the repeated-measurement model

\[
v_y^{(a)}=s_y+\eta_y^{(a)},
\]

where \(s_y\) is identity-reproducible visual evidence and the two errors—photograph-specific background, pose, crop, illumination, and augmentation response—are conditionally independent with zero mean given \(y\). Then

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(v^{(1)},v^{(2)})
=\operatorname{Cov}(s_y),
\]

while

\[
\operatorname{Cov}(v^{(a)})
=\operatorname{Cov}(s_y)+\operatorname{Cov}(\eta^{(a)}).
\]

Consequently, the singular/eigen spectrum of \(C\) consists of generalized reliability ratios. A direction containing only independent instance or augmentation noise has cross-covariance zero and reliability zero. Importantly, two augmented views of the *same photograph* do not have this property: their cross-covariance also retains the photograph-specific term. That is the causal reason for requiring distinct photographs.

Cheap degeneracies are excluded as follows:

- **Constant collapse:** \(S_{12}=0\), hence \(L_{\rm CRS}=-\log(10^{-3})\), while PFML’s repulsive energy is also unsatisfied.
- **Rank-\(r<16\) collapse:** for almost every random \(R\), \(\operatorname{rank}(C)\le r\); at least \(16-r\) determinant factors remain at \(10^{-3}\). A trace objective could hide these zeros, but the log determinant cannot.
- **Pure image/augmentation code:** conditional independence makes its population \(S_{12}=0\); allocating normalized-descriptor norm to it worsens both CRS and usable PFML separation.
- **Norm inflation:** impossible because both embeddings and proxies are normalized; whitening also makes CRS scale invariant.
- **Coordinate rotation or permutation:** harmless because random subspaces and singular values are rotationally invariant.

This does **not** rule out a training-identity lookup code or a background consistently shared by every photograph of one training identity. Such a feature is repeatable and can pass CRS. The hypothesis is narrower: removing nonrepeatable high-margin directions frees capacity for reusable morphology. Static identity-correlated shortcuts remain a genuine failure mode.

### 3. Adversarial novelty search

Search cutoff: 2026-08-05. I searched DML, supervised/contrastive learning, repeated-measurement models, CCA, augmentation invariance, covariance regularization, and domain generalization. I found close ingredients, but not the proposed combination of distinct-photo cross-fitting, randomized low-dimensional canonical spectra, a weakest-spectrum log determinant, and PFML in zero-shot DML.

- [Hotelling’s canonical correlation analysis](https://academic.oup.com/biomet/article-abstract/28/3-4/321/220073) finds linear relations between two fixed variable sets; CRS trains a nonlinear descriptor from randomly paired repeated identity measurements and repeatedly probes the full descriptor with random subspaces.
- [Barlow Twins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03230) drives coordinatewise cross-correlation toward identity for two augmentations of the *same image*; CRS uses distinct photographs, full whitening, and a rotation-invariant reliability-spectrum determinant.
- Supervised contrastive learning attracts all same-label pairs through pairwise log-softmax forces; CRS estimates the repeatable covariance component and penalizes missing reliable rank rather than summing positive similarities.
- [Deep Variational Metric Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ECCV_2018/html/Xudong_Lin_Deep_Variational_Metric_ECCV_2018_paper.html) models and generates class-independent intra-class variation; CRS neither fits a latent density nor generates features.
- [Deep Compositional Metric Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/html/Zheng_Deep_Compositional_Metric_Learning_CVPR_2021_paper.html) learns several subembeddings and compositors; CRS deploys one undivided 512-D descriptor and random projections exist only inside its training loss.
- [Deep Meta Metric Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ICCV_2019/html/Chen_Deep_Meta_Metric_Learning_ICCV_2019_paper.html) learns support/query set distances over sampled tasks; CRS performs no inner loop or set-valued inference and directly estimates repeated-measure reliability.
- [DADA](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/29400) aligns sample and proxy distributions through domain adaptation; CRS does not classify domains or align proxy distributions.
- [PFML](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Bhatnagar_Potential_Field_Based_Deep_Metric_Learning_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf) supplies local decaying attractive/repulsive forces, but has no cross-fitted repeatability estimator or spectral-rank pressure.

The main novelty vulnerability is that a reviewer may classify CRS as supervised CCA/Barlow-style redundancy reduction applied to same-class photographs. The strongest distinction is not “cross-correlation plus PFML”; it is the repeated-measurement estimand and log-product of randomized canonical reliabilities.

### 4. Decisive controls

Every control uses the same ResNet, 512 dimensions, optimizer, images, augmentations, batch composition, proxies, 200 epochs, and five seeds.

1. **PFML alone:** establishes the matched frontier baseline.
2. **Same-image CRS:** replace the second photograph with a second augmentation of the first. If this matches CRS, the claimed removal of photograph-specific shortcuts is unsupported.
3. **Positive MSE:** replace CRS with \(\|z_y^{(1)}-z_y^{(2)}\|^2\), normalized to the same EMA loss weight. This tests ordinary invariance/compactness.
4. **Barlow control:** target the unwhitened coordinate correlation matrix to \(I\), using the same distinct photographs and compute.
5. **Trace control:** replace \(-\log\det(CC^\top+\epsilon I)\) with \(-\operatorname{tr}(CC^\top)/16\). This distinguishes distributed reliable rank from average correlation.
6. **Pair-shuffle negative control:** permute branch-2 identities before forming \(S_{12}\). It should erase both the spectral improvement and retrieval gain.
7. **Fixed-subspace control:** retain one \(R\) throughout training. This tests whether coverage of all 512 dimensions, rather than merely adding a 16-D auxiliary head, matters.

Mechanistic support requires distinct-photo CRS to beat same-image CRS, positive MSE, and the trace control by at least \(0.2\) R@1 points averaged over CUB and Cars, while pair-shuffling eliminates at least 80% of its gain.

### 6. Cost and risks

Training uses the same 64 image forwards and PFML proxies as its matched baseline. The extra work is two \(16\times16\) eigendecompositions/matrix operations and a \(512\times16\) projection: expected overhead is below 1% FLOPs, about 1–3% wall time, and under 1 MB working memory. Deployment cost and storage are exactly unchanged.

The largest risks are:

- SOP’s few photographs per identity make its repeated-measure covariance much noisier, so the SOP forecast is less mechanistically secure.
- Strong crops can destroy identity evidence, violating the conditional repeated-measure model.
- Stable per-identity backgrounds survive CRS.
- The log determinant can overemphasize weak/noisy directions; \(q=16\), ridge \(10^{-3}\), and the 0.1 loss weight are intended to limit that.
- PFML’s proxies and training-label count make training memory dataset-dependent even though deployment is fixed.
- ImageNet-1K initialization may contain visually related bird or vehicle categories. It is expressly permitted here, but exact pretrained checkpoint hashes must be recorded.
- Hyperparameter selection must use only identity-disjoint validation folds carved from the official training identities, followed by one retraining on all official training identities. Test images must never influence schedules, augmentation, early stopping, or method selection.
