# VERDICT: LIVE

**Single strongest decisive reason.** Every independently checkable element of the mechanism core survives: the symmetric Sinkhorn mathematics (existence, uniqueness, fixed point, batch-level balance certificate) is correct as written; the specific composite — a supervised batch retrieval likelihood normalized under the symmetric doubly-stochastic coupling, plus a variance-of-potentials penalty forcing the *raw deployed* kernel toward balance, deployed as plain 512-D cosine NN — has no occupant I could find in primary literature (the nearest neighbors are inference-side doubly-stochastic rescoring and non-balancing train-time hub losses); and the frozen plan contains a genuine matched-compute killer control (C5) plus cheap pre-registered kill switches (C7, C6, the Δ-ordering falsifier) that make the causal claim adjudicable and self-terminating if wrong. A sound, unoccupied, self-falsifying mechanism is what a live candidate looks like, even though several of its quantitative claims are inflated (detailed below).

## 1. Mathematics — verified, with two overstatements

- **Existence/uniqueness**: for B ≥ 3, a symmetric kernel with zero diagonal and strictly positive off-diagonal is fully indecomposable (any zero block R×C would have to lie inside the diagonal, forcing |R|=|C|=1 and B=2), so a unique symmetric positive scaling to doubly stochastic exists (Sinkhorn–Knopp; [Knight 2008](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/060659624)). Correct.
- **Iteration**: the damped log-domain update has exactly the stated fixed point sᵢΣⱼK_ijsⱼ=1; damping is indeed required in the symmetric case. "Converges to ‖P𝟙−𝟙‖∞<1e-3 in ≤15 iterations" is an *empirical* assertion, not proven — worst-case Hilbert-metric contraction at τ_b=0.1 can be slow — but it is monitored with a stated guard, so it is honest.
- **Lemma 1**: the block-uniform scaling s=((M−1)e^{1/τ})^{−1/2} checks out; both new terms vanish in the perfect-clustering limit, and the fixed-point separation from Wang–Isola uniformity is real. Two caveats: "maximally violated" is rhetoric (uniformity's maximal violation is total collapse), and the lemma's zero depends on the sampler's *equal class sizes* — under unequal test-gallery class sizes, exact raw-kernel balance and perfect clustering are no longer simultaneously satisfiable. Neither breaks the argument's use.
- **Proposition 3**: both directions verified (constant a ⟺ constant row sums; symmetry gives column-stochasticity of the row softmax). But "deployment certificate — exact" overstates scope: it is exact for the *training batch distribution, at temperature τ_b, in soft mass*. Reaching actual deployment requires three extrapolations — train→unseen-class shift, batch-mixture→gallery composition, soft mass→hard rank-1 — of which the proposal names two (Degeneracies C, D) and leaves the class-shift step implicit.
- **Arithmetic spot-checks**: Degeneracy B's log(89/2)≈3.8 ✓; the hub-penalty gradient channel through −(aᵢ+aⱼ) is real ✓; compute claim (<3% overhead, 15·B² LSEs vs a ResNet-50 batch-90 step) trivially true ✓. One factual slip: Cars196's test split has **98** classes (~83 images each), not "49 dense classes" — the density rationale for the Δ-ordering survives, the number is wrong.

## 2. Does batch balancing constrain the deployed kernel?

Scaling absorption is genuinely closed (potentials are deterministic functions of embeddings; L_gap's zero is exactly raw balance). Uniform collapse is closed by the likelihood term. Class/block shortcuts are moot for R@1. Two residuals remain: (a) an **unlisted compensation channel** — at finite loss a point can stay an impostor hub by carrying less within-class mass, since only the *total* row mass is constrained under the equal-M sampler; this closes only asymptotically as L_bal and L_gap jointly →0; (b) the batch→gallery mixture shift (Degeneracy C, declared). Both consequences land on test-gallery N₁ statistics, which C6 measures directly, and C5 protects attribution. Heuristic rather than provable transfer — but declared, instrumented, and falsifiable. Acceptable.

## 3. Hubness causality and the C7 gate

The premise magnitude is unmeasured on these benchmarks, as the proposal admits. The sentence "**its gain upper-bounds internalizable headroom**" is logically unsound in both directions: CSLS rescores the *baseline's* frozen geometry, while retraining reshapes geometry throughout learning (can exceed rescoring), and CSLS consumes gallery statistics unavailable to a deployment-legal method (can exceed what is internalizable). C7 is defensible only as a *premise gate* (small CSLS gain ⇒ little hub-recoverable error), which is the role its kill threshold actually plays. C6's attribution (wrong item in top-5% N₁) is confounded by legitimately central/duplicate items — flagged for SOP, real for all four.

## 4. Prior art — thesis sentence false; mechanism slot still open

The proposal's characterizations of noHub ([CVPR 2023](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.09352), transductive/inference), the [Neurocomputing 2025 uniformity approach](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0925231225015516) (train-time, clustering-conflicted), and SinSim ([arXiv 2502.10478](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.10478v1) — verified: an entropic-OT *distance* regularizer added to NT-Xent, no doubly-stochastic constraint, no raw-kernel penalty, no hubness motivation) are accurate. However:

- **"All published fixes for this live at inference" is false.** [HAL, AAAI 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.10097) trains a hubness-aware loss (NCA-based local/global hub reweighting) for text-image matching, and [DeHub, ICMR 2026](https://doi.org/10.1145/3805622.3810620) is explicitly a training-phase hub-resistant representation framework for text-video retrieval. Both are cross-modal, neither is balancing-based, but the blanket claim — and the rhetorical premium built on it — does not stand.
- **The exact operator is already published inference-side**: [Dual Bank Sinkhorn Normalization, arXiv 2508.02538](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02538) (Aug 2025) performs doubly-stochastic Sinkhorn balancing of retrieval probabilities for hubness reduction — confirmed plug-in, post-hoc, bank-based at test time. This simultaneously *strengthens the premise* (published evidence that DS balancing of retrieval mass reduces hubness) and *narrows the novelty*: SBE's contribution is the train-time internalization with the raw-kernel gap certificate in supervised DML, not the balancing idea itself.
- I found no occupant of the narrowed slot across DML, supervised contrastive learning, doubly-stochastic affinity learning ([bi-stochastic similarity work](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220764912_Learning_a_Bi-Stochastic_Data_Similarity_Matrix) is post-hoc on fixed data), OT, or SSL (SwAV-family balances instance×prototype assignments, not the deployed instance kernel). The RCSLS lineage remains the closest ancestor, as the proposal says. Caveat: I am blind by design to earlier frozen proposals in this series; that collision check is outside my evidence base.

## 5. Controls

C5 (row-softmax at matched τ, λ, compute) is exactly the right instrument to separate the column constraint from "just added SupCon," and C2 centering is the correct first-order hub control given the hubness–centroid link. C4/C5 triangulate the gap term. Two omissions: no **gap-only arm** (PA + Var(a) alone ≈ log-row-sum equalization — a cheap candidate for the whole effect), and no batch-size/composition sensitivity arm (constraint quality scales with B and the equal-M mixture). Weakens attribution at the margins; does not break it.

## 6. Forecasts — the weakest axis

Lane discipline is correct (refs match the rubric; no 2048-D/transformer mixing; In-Shop CI arithmetic 93.5−0.3=93.2>93.0 checks). But: (i) the PA baselines are **assumed, not measured** — Cars 91.4 vs 86.1 published PA is the most exposed (+5.3 assumed reproduction gain), and crossings inherit that assumption; (ii) the central forecasts don't follow from the registered premises: hub share ≥15% of SOP errors × ≥30% reduction × 19-pt error rate ≈ **+0.9 R@1**, versus the +2.2 headline — a ~2.5× gap that could only be filled by non-hub channels, which falsifier (e) itself disallows. The proposal's own hedges (SOP "borderline, P≈0.5"; CUB/Cars "ties-to-marginal") and its falsification thresholds are coherent and would correctly kill the frontier claim if the C6-minimum world is the real one. Optimistic, honestly labeled, self-adjudicating.

## 7. Hidden risks

None found beyond the declared set: no test-time statistics, clean class-disjoint tuning with retraining, final-checkpoint evaluation, 5 seeds, matched wall-clock, no auxiliary networks, inert clamp, standard frozen-BN practice. Minor: τ_b/λ_g selected on a 15%-of-classes split (15 classes on CUB) is noisy model selection.

**Bottom line**: the thesis sentence is falsified, the certificate and C7 claims are overstated, and the headline forecasts exceed what the registered premises support — but the mechanism mathematics is verified, the slot is unoccupied in the public record, the controls can distinguish the claimed mechanism from every named confound, and the frozen kill switches would catch each identified failure mode cheaply. **LIVE.**
