## Verdict

**DEAD at Gates 1 and 2, independently.** The exact certificate is only a
lower bound on *training-proxy difference rank*, a condition already satisfied
by generic one-proxy-per-class tables and disconnected from transferable image
attributes. The supervision action is an exact recurrence of Pass 29 DSA and
the established representation-self-challenging/adversarial-erasure family.
No diagnostic, preregistration, implementation, or candidate GPU is warranted.

## Gate 1: no measured rank-starvation provenance

AXE assumes that corrected zero-shot retrieval errors are caused by training
identity evidence occupying too few descriptor directions, that worst-case
subspace deletion measures that failure, and that making seen labels survive
the deletion computes additional transferable attributes. No repository
measurement establishes any link in that chain.

The proposal's claimed CUB base participation rank `8--15`, class-count dose,
random-orientation unseen differences, and `+1.9/+1.6` CUB/Cars gains are
invented forecasts. The algebraic ceiling `rank <= C-1` is not a measurement
of an active bottleneck. Candidate 371 already recorded that supervised-rank
versus demanded-rank arithmetic supplies no benchmark dimension requirement.
Pass 29 DSA made the same unmeasured causal substitution and was rejected.

There is adverse evidence for the transfer premise. Candidate 225's
prospective disjoint-identity subspace-transfer ratios were `0.9312`, `0.9287`,
and `0.9345`, all below the locked `1.15` threshold: leading within-class
directions learned on source identities captured no preferential target
within-class structure. That statistic is not AXE's between-class object, so
it does not prove the opposite sign, but it directly denies the proposal's
unsupported claim that training-identity subspace manipulation reveals shared
unseen-identity attributes.

The proposed C12 class-count sweep is a future mechanism test, not provenance.
It also changes which identities and visual distinctions exist while holding
an image budget, so a class-count trend would not isolate rank starvation.

## The certificate does not certify the claimed representation

Proposition 2 is a valid one-way statement about the affine span of the
**learned proxy table** under an exact global inner maximum: if all proxy
differences lie in a deleted `k`-space, surviving proxy logits are class-
constant and PA has a positive floor. Its contrapositive establishes only

```
rank(span{p_c-p_c'}) >= k+1.
```

It says nothing about class-mean feature rank, attribute rank, unseen-identity
information, or robustness of deployed image-to-image retrieval. For one
proxy per class, a generic random table already has difference rank
`min(C-1,d)`: 99 on CUB, 97 on Cars, and 512 on SOP/In-Shop. Thus the proposed
`k=32--48` certificate is satisfied by the ordinary proxy parameterization
before AXE learns any new image evidence. On SOP/In-Shop it is algebraically
vacuous for every proposed `k<512`.

The proxy shortcut is operational, not merely logical. Proxies receive a
100-times learning rate and can distribute themselves across many directions
while the shared encoder continues using a low-dimensional class code. AXE
projects and optimizes the proxies in the auxiliary term, so proxy motion can
improve every registered certificate statistic without creating one new
transferable feature. C9 replaces proxies by random vectors and shuffled
labels; it does not hold learned proxies fixed or isolate their high-rate
optimization. C5 measures proxy scatter and therefore repeats the same
shortcut rather than controlling it.

The proposal itself concedes that its multi-proxy frontier arm makes the proxy
certificate vacuous, then suggests restating it on feature means without
defining or freezing that new method. The only nominal crossing therefore
depends on an object for which the stated theorem does not apply.

## The approximate adversary cannot support the universal claim

The headline says *every* codimension-`k` surviving subspace retains the task.
The executable training objective follows three persistent frames for two
normalized ascent steps, periodically replacing only the weakest frame. That
is a heuristic lower bound on a high-dimensional Grassmann maximum, not the
universal maximum in Proposition 2.

F6 does not close the gap. Sixty-four fresh Haar subspaces in
`Gr(k,512)` have negligible probability of aligning with an unknown narrow
worst-case signal span. Comparing their losses to three locally optimized
buffers tests typical random deletion, not the global worst-case deletion.
Both values can lie below `3.24` while an unvisited adversarial subspace remains
above it. Logging an approximate maximum against an exact theorem's threshold
does not turn the lower bound into a certificate.

The objective also permits a high-rank seen-class code with no semantic
factorization. Rotation invariance blocks coordinate duplication of one linear
feature, but not nonlinear recoding or identity lookup into many independent
directions. The proposal's defence is only an implicit-bias story plus a
train-versus-held-out gap. A class code can leave that aggregate gap unchanged
while still failing to correspond to reusable attributes; no cross-identity
factor correspondence is supervised.

## Gate 2: exact internal recurrence and occupied public action

Pass 29 Discriminant-Subspace Ablation already estimated the currently
discriminative training-identity subspace, deleted it from embeddings and
proxies, and reapplied Proxy Anchor to force label prediction from residual
directions. AXE replaces the leading-scatter selector by a loss-maximizing
Stiefel selector and adds persistent buffers. That changes the erasure
estimator, not the supervision action: **delete the strongest seen-label
features and optimize the same label loss on what survives**.

Candidate 157 had already rejected requiring identity evidence to survive
feature erasure as feature-dropout consistency, candidate 365 rejected
proxy-complement allocation without provenance, and Passes 30, 39, 40, and 41
closed nearby rank/capacity/redundancy constraints. AXE's own C2 names its
axis-aligned limit “embedding adversarial dropout,” and its C5 concedes that a
spectral rank penalty may reproduce the mechanism.

Publicly, Representation Self-Challenging (Huang et al., ECCV 2020) iteratively
discards the dominant label-correlated representation features and trains the
same task from remaining features to improve out-of-domain generalization.
Adversarial Dropout chooses a worst-case feature/unit mask and trains the
reconfigured network; adversarial erasing and feature-erasure ReID methods use
the same make-alternative-evidence-survive action. BIER and DML ensembles
explicitly distribute retrieval evidence across components. A continuous
rotation-invariant Stiefel mask and a proxy-rank floor are narrower wrappers,
not a new supervision referent.

Primary sources:

- RSC: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.02454>
- Adversarial Dropout: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.03631>
- Adversarial Dropout Regularization: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.01575>
- Domain Guided Dropout for ReID: <https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2016/html/Xiao_Learning_Deep_Feature_CVPR_2016_paper.html>
- Adversarially Occluded Samples for ReID: <https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Huang_Adversarially_Occluded_Samples_CVPR_2018_paper.html>

## Recipe, controls, and standing objective

The proposal violates the repository's screening order by making a multi-run
CUB class-count experiment its first run and by forecasting In-Shop as a
secondary `+0.8` effect. Corrected paired In-Shop must be the first candidate
screen after Gates 1--3. Its constructed ResNet-50 SOP/In-Shop recipe does not
match the corrected local BN-Inception reference, and it omits raw-best versus
independently selected/final reporting and selection-bias analysis.

The hyperparameter budget is large: two dataset-specific `k` grids, four
lambda values, three weight decays, adversary count, steps, radius, reset
interval, rank schedule, and an SOP proxy-sampling fallback. A class-disjoint
fold is appropriate, but the matched baseline does not receive an equivalent
structural search. C8's extra baseline steps cannot match the optimization
effect of six inner adversary gradients plus a second PA backward merely by
adding three percent wall clock.

Standalone AXE is forecast below every audited Lane-A frontier. Its CUB
multi-proxy mean `0.736` is below its own decisive `0.740` threshold; Cars,
SOP, and In-Shop miss. The only suggested SOTA comes from adding the forecast
delta to an unreproduced PFML carrier, explicitly labelled arithmetic rather
than a forecast. Matching the frozen numbers would not fulfill the standing
objective.

## Correct pieces worth preserving

- Subspace-restricted cosine computed by subtracting `UU^T` components is
  coordinate-free and deployment-clean.
- The exact global-minimax proxy-rank contrapositive is valid in its narrow
  stated domain.
- Projecting queries and proxies avoids a query-only common-mean shrinkage
  shortcut.
- Positive rescaling of features/proxies does not change restricted cosine.
- Random, axis-aligned, fixed-frame, spectral, uniformity, compute, and
  query-only controls are useful engineering comparisons.
- The proposal states its non-crossing forecasts, certificate approximation,
  memorization risk, and source ambiguities unusually candidly.

## Mechanism lesson

A certificate on trainable proxy rank is not a certificate on image evidence.
Before any redundancy/rank proposal reaches GPU, freeze which object carries
the claimed information, prove auxiliaries cannot satisfy the statistic while
the deployed encoder remains unchanged, and distinguish a true global
worst-case from a handful of typical or locally optimized subspaces. Changing
the mask from coordinate-wise to Stiefel does not reopen the already occupied
self-challenging supervision action.
