# Re-audit of rejected searches after protocol repair (2026-08-08)

The protocol repair requires rechecking earlier negatives.  I classified the
ledger by the *first dispositive reason*, rather than treating every `NONE` as
an empirical result.

## Reopened as non-evidence

Passes 58, 59, 60, 61, 64, 66, 67, 69, 70, 71, and 72 were blind-proposer
`NONE` passes with no concrete executable method that could be preregistered.
Several explicitly acknowledged the contradiction: the proposer was blind to
repository measurements while Gate 1 required measurement provenance.  Their
closure lemmas and literature notes are useful hypotheses, but they do not
prove that an unseen method is absent.  They are reopened through the repaired
measurement-conditioned lane and the independent blind lane; no historical
GPU result needs to be rerun because no method was ever implemented.

Pass 60's CUB whitening/alpha probes remain descriptive post-hoc diagnostics,
not method tests.  Pass 73 later corrected the In-Shop error-ratio premise, so
the CUB-only closure is not reused.

## Still valid negatives

The following are not invalidated by the discovery-gate defect:

* Gate-0 artifact, split, checkpoint, or implementation failures (for example
  Passes 74, 105, 119/120 closeout, and the SRC audit);
* preregistered CPU falsifiers with correctly recomputed artifacts (Passes 62,
  64 alpha probe, 105 support escape, 149/150/153/154, and 157);
* exact mechanism-equivalent prior-art reviews after a concrete proposal
  existed (Passes 63, 65, 68, 73, 76–104, 107–118, 121–147, and 155–163);
* matched GPU screen failures or implementation defects (EMA/RSPG/ARCG/CIS
  and the other explicitly run arms).

These remain closed only for their tested mechanism, not for every broad
family.  Adjacent rather than equivalent work is now `LIVE-NARROW` under the
repaired Gate 2.

## Consequence

The historical count of “failed candidates” overstates the evidence: a material
subset were proposer `NONE` responses, not rejected experiments.  The search is
therefore reopened, but GPU work still requires a concrete proposal, primary
prior-art audit, corrected four-seed forecast, and a CPU falsifier that measures
the deployed cosine geometry where possible.
