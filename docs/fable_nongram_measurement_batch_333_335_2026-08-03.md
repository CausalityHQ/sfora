# Fable non-Gram measurement batch 333--335

Date: 2026-08-03. Generated by a read-only Fable 5 pass while the repaired PFML
Cars reference was still running, then checked against the repository catalogue
and primary-source landing pages. No diagnostic, implementation, or GPU work.

## Result

**No measurement survives Gate 2.** With one final checkpoint, final
embeddings/proxies, raw images, labels, and scalar curves, the available
observables fall into four covered classes:

1. embedding/proxy/label geometry is output-Gram-derived;
2. modified-input responses are augmentation, equivariance, Jacobian,
   attribution, local-evidence, or reconstruction signals;
3. pixel/file groupings become clustering, pseudo-labels, acquisition-domain
   conditioning, or invariance;
4. aggregate curves contain no per-sample trajectory relation.

The only structural escape is a new observation channel not already contained
in pixels, class labels, or the trained map, or an explicit task/deployment
change. The current artifact set supplies neither.

## 333. Negative-control nuisance orthogonality

**Proposed measurement.** Extract model-free JPEG quantization/resolution and
global log-chromaticity statistics from Cars training files. Within each class,
compare nuisance distance with final embedding distance and retrieval error.
The prospective evidentiary threshold would have been Kendall tau at least 0.25
in at least 40% of eligible classes plus at least 2x error enrichment in the
top nuisance-distance decile.

**Why it dies.** No verified Cars measurement presently motivates those
particular acquisition variables, so Gate 1 fails. Even if the threshold passed,
the executable relation would make within-class residual embeddings independent
of an observed nuisance. Its covariance/HSIC-style gradient is a
nuisance-coefficient-weighted recombination of existing embedding atoms. That is
observed-domain/nuisance invariance, not a new supervision primitive. MIC already
learns/removes interclass characteristics in DML; VFAE is an established observed-
nuisance invariance construction. The proximal-negative-control analogy does not
rescue novelty: proximal identification needs additional proxy channels for an
unobserved confounder, whereas this proposal has one observed nuisance statistic.

**Verdict: DEAD at Gates 1 and 2.** The statistic could be a data audit, but it
cannot authorize a novel method.

## 334. Repeat-visit detectability

**Proposed measurement.** Import ecological occupancy/detection modeling by
treating a class as a site and its images as repeat visits. Fit a latent
per-image probability that identity evidence is detectable from within-class
nearest-neighbor agreement. A prospective threshold would require a probability
spread of at least 0.3 within at least 30% of classes.

**Why it dies.** The agreement pattern, likelihood, and fitted detectability are
entirely functions of final embeddings and labels, hence output-Gram-derived.
Routing detectability into training is either a scalar pair weight or a hard
censoring mask. Candidate 328 already reduced censored pairs to hinge/listwise
supervision; candidate 327 established that a new estimator for an occupied
operator is insufficient. Estimating the target from the same model also makes
it endogenous, repeating the failure of self-generated eligibility graphs.

**Verdict: DEAD at the non-Gram authorization bar and Gate 2.** MacKenzie et
al.'s occupancy model is a legitimate estimator in ecology; moving it to DML
does not make the resulting weighting/censoring operator novel.

## 335. Class-name lattice supervision

**Proposed measurement.** Parse Cars class names into make/model/year or body-
type relations and measure whether confusions concentrate along the resulting
class lattice. A proposed threshold was at least 3x shared-make enrichment under
10,000 degree-preserving name permutations, with a monotone hierarchy effect.

**Why it dies.** The prediction is nearly foregone for a fine-grained car
dataset, so it is not a useful falsifier. More importantly, the authorized
relation is an ordinal or hierarchical target between class pairs. Its gradient
is the same attraction/repulsion atom with a graded coefficient. Conditional
Similarity Networks, attribute-specific similarity learning, and hierarchical
DML already occupy the operator. Extracting the hierarchy from a class-name
string rather than an ontology changes the metadata source, not the mechanism;
it also has no equivalent for opaque In-Shop identities.

**Verdict: DEAD at Gates 1 and 2.** This would change the annotation channel and
benchmark claim without opening a new similarity-learning operator.

## Other cross-field imports killed in reduction

- Neuroscience information-limiting correlations and manifold capacity are
  Gram-derived covariance/capacity diagnostics; acting on them is covariance,
  Fisher, coding-rate, or capacity regularization.
- PFML force balance is the embedding-space loss gradient itself; acting on it
  is stopping, adaptive margins, or weighting.
- Sensor PRNU is a real pixel/file channel, but it authorizes only camera/source
  invariance.
- Embedding chirality is not fixed by the Gram because reflection changes it,
  but cosine retrieval is invariant to that gauge. Fixing the frame is occupied
  gauge fixing and cannot improve the evaluated geometry directly.
