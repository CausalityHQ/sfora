# In-Shop true lower-stack freeze gate, 26 September 2026

The earlier lower-12-block freeze left SigLIP2 patch and position embeddings trainable, forcing backward activation propagation through frozen blocks. Its three-seed quality replication failed one predeclared per-seed Recall@1 interval, despite positive paired mean mAP@R and 14.1% lower mean training wall. A new `freeze_emb` arm additionally freezes the vision embedding module. No source, architecture, data, rank loss, learning rate, schedule, scorer, or deployed format changes. The post-layernorm and pooling head remain trainable. This tests whether cutting the entire lower-stack backward path materially improves training cost while retaining the matched full-control quality. It does not resolve the official In-Shop quality gap.

Frozen before treatment results, seed 179024, official In-Shop **TRAIN-only** product-disjoint 13,283-fit/12,599-held split:

1. Run 17-update old `freeze` replay and `freeze_emb` treatment serially on the sole DGX Spark. The replay must exactly match the archived seed-179024 freeze smoke's 400 vision tensors, two head tensors, classifier, first/last losses and 17 preclip norms. Paired model, split, schedule, first-ten inputs, PCA and shared source-file hashes must match. The new arm must leave every embedding and lower-block tensor equal to the pinned pretrained checkpoint, while changing trainable upper layers, head and classifier. All 17 updates must be finite. Advance only if treatment wall **including bank initialization** and peak allocated CUDA are each at least 10% below the replay. This is a cost smoke, not a quality read.
2. If smoke passes, run one 1,000-update treatment and score the existing 12,599-image self-excluded held gallery with the exact 128-D packed scorer. Compare to the archived full seed-179024 control (R@1 98.5554%, mAP@R 0.826739, wall1,147.928s, peak22.554GB) and archived old freeze (R@1 98.6427%, mAP@R 0.837651, wall984.556s, peak18.123GB). Advance only if treatment packed R@1 point is at least control, the paired product-bootstrap 95% lower bound versus control is greater than **−0.30 percentage points**, mAP@R is at least control, and training wall and peak CUDA are each at least 10% below old freeze. Require 1,000 finite updates, source/checkpoint/receipt hashes and exact per-query vectors. Stop before another seed if any gate fails; do not read official TEST.
3. A passing seed is exploratory. Predeclare fresh independent seeds and a seed-aware uncertainty gate before promoting a production training option. The existing three frozen seeds and this reused seed cannot by themselves establish official quality or an algorithmic improvement.

The 10% cost threshold is larger than routine smoke timing jitter and asks for a material improvement beyond the earlier freeze. The R@1 noninferiority allowance applies only to the interval; the point estimate and mAP@R must meet or exceed full control. This is a cost-oriented candidate, not a claim of improved retrieval quality. All production defaults remain on the existing control unless independent confirmation passes.
