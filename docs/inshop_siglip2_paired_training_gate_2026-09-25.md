The paired arms use the same SigLIP2 Large/256 backbone, 128-D PCA-initialized head, fit-only ArcFace proxies, BF16 vision autocast, AdamW rates and decay, 1,000 updates × 64 images, coverage-first schedule, 256-pixel augmentation, and packed 130-byte int8 scorer. The bank arm uses the unchanged SOP coefficient 8.0 and detached full-fit SmoothAP bank; the fixed float arm uses the unchanged SOP coefficient 21.93 and in-batch float SmoothAP. Both skip the rank term for an entire batch containing a singleton fit product while retaining ArcFace; bank refresh continues. The only bank speed change removes trailing invalid positive padding and is algebraically exact.

Freeze seed order `179023`, `179024`, `179025`; run bank then float serially within each seed. Each run exports all TRAIN images at batch 64 and scores the held TRAIN queries against the full TRAIN gallery, excluding self, in packed-score arithmetic. Compare three-seed paired Recall@1 and mAP@R, report all seeds and training wall/throughput/peak VRAM. A single seed or TRAIN holdout cannot establish official In-Shop quality. Do not tune using official query/gallery. A failed source/finite/cost check stops the campaign for diagnosis; a low quality number is reported, not silently repaired or discarded. Numerical superiority alone is not a state-of-the-art claim.
